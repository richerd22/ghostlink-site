GETTING STARTED

From the box to your first night. Eight steps, one thing each. Doing it at home first is a good idea: skip step 2 until you're at the booth.

BOO: Hi, I'm BOO, the ghost in the box. I'll chip in now and then. Every step works without me.

  1. WHAT'S IN THE BOX

    The GHOST LINK box and its own power supply. You also need a network cable long enough to reach the DJ switch, and your phone. That's all it takes to start.

    Extras (a sound card for recordings, a MIDI interface for a lighting desk) are optional: gear to buy.

  2. PLUG INTO THE DJ NETWORK

    At the booth, run the network cable from the box to a free port on the switch or DJ router the players and mixer use. Not straight into one player: then it only sees that one.

    No free port? A small plain switch fixes that: how.

  3. POWER

    Plug in the box's own supply: the official Raspberry Pi 27W USB-C supply (5.1V, 5A). Push the plug all the way in. A phone charger is often too weak, and the box will say Power low.

    Give it a minute. It finds the players and the mixer by itself.

    BOO: Power in is all it takes. I wake up by myself.

  4. JOIN THE WI-FI

    First time, or anywhere it doesn't know: join the Wi-Fi called GHOST LINK on your phone. The password came with your box (on the sticker or card).

    Once the box knows the venue's Wi-Fi (step 7), it joins that by itself. Then just stay on the venue Wi-Fi.

  5. OPEN THE DASHBOARD

    On the GHOST LINK Wi-Fi the dashboard pops up by itself: tap GOT IT. If it doesn't, open 10.42.0.1 or scan the OPEN code on the sticker.

    On a venue Wi-Fi it knows: open ghostlink.local or gl.cold.fyi, or tap GHOST LINK at the top of a phone that already has it open for a QR. No app needed, any phone browser.

    Using the HARD STOP app? It finds GHOST LINK on the same Wi-Fi once its next update is out: using it with HARD STOP.

  6. SET YOUR PASSWORD

    STATUS, type the password that came with your box, UNLOCK. Then CHANGE PASSWORD to one of your own, 8 characters or more.

    Watching is open to anyone on the Wi-Fi. Changing things needs the password. For crew, give tonight's 4-digit crew code instead.

  7. ADD YOUR VENUE AND ITS WI-FI

    STATUS → VENUE → NEW. Name it (say, THE ROLLER RINK), then + VENUE WI-FI with the Wi-Fi name and password exactly as the venue gave them, capitals and spaces included. SAVE.

    Do the same with your home Wi-Fi: a venue called HOME. The box joins the strongest one it knows, wherever it is. Passwords stay on the box.

  8. PLAN A NIGHT

    STATUS → NIGHTS → PLAN A NIGHT: the date, the venue, STARTS, MUSIC OFF and the lineup, one line each like 22:00 TICK.

    On the night, tracks land under the right DJ by themselves. Optional, but it makes PLAYED tidy.

    BOO: TICK at ten, LIL TICK at midnight, TICK JR closing. I'll keep their tracklists apart.

YOUR FIRST NIGHT

Before doors, top to bottom. Tick as you go:

  • Cable from the box to the DJ switch, then power.
  • Green light on the box: solid = on a Wi-Fi it knows, slow blink = it is the GHOST LINK Wi-Fi.
  • Your phone on the same Wi-Fi, dashboard open.
  • STATUS → BOOTH CHECK: read every line. Confirm the venue if it asks.
  • Load a track on each deck: title, art and waveform show on DECKS.
  • Share: tap GHOST LINK at the top and show the QR to the crew.
  • Recording a set, lights, or a hard curfew tonight? Only if wanted: the optional steps.

During the night there's nothing to do. If anything looks odd, STATUS → LISTEN ONLY, no password needed. At the end of the night, just unplug it. Nothing breaks.

BOO: That's it. Go enjoy the night. I'll keep watch.
