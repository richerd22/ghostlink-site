FIRST NIGHT AT A NEW VENUE

One page to print. Tick as you go.

BEFORE THE DAY

  • ☐ Ask the venue for its Wi-Fi name and password, if they'll share it.
  • ☐ Ask if the DJ router or switch has a free port. If not, bring a small switch.
  • ☐ Plan the night: STATUS → NIGHTS → PLAN A NIGHT. Date, venue, STARTS, MUSIC OFF, the lineup. How.
  • ☐ Optional: STATUS → VENUE → EDIT → + VENUE WI-FI now, so the box joins by itself on arrival.

BRING

  • ☐ The box and its own power supply.
  • ☐ A network cable, and a spare.
  • ☐ A small switch, in case the router is full.
  • ☐ Only if you'll use them: the USB sound card and RCA cable, the USB MIDI interface. Gear.

AT THE BOOTH

  • ☐ Cable from the box to the switch or DJ router the players use. Never into one player.
  • ☐ Power. Wait a minute. Green light: solid = on a Wi-Fi it knows; slow blink = join GHOST LINK.
  • ☐ Phone on the same Wi-Fi. Open the dashboard.
  • ☐ New Wi-Fi here? STATUS → VENUE → EDIT → + VENUE WI-FI. Next time it joins by itself. How.
  • ☐ STATUS → BOOTH CHECK, top to bottom. VENUE right? DJ CABLE IN, JOINED AS DEVICE (7 to 15), all your PLAYERS, the MIXER.
  • ☐ Optional: STATUS → TOOLS → TEST RUN. Leave it open; it ticks itself.
  • ☐ Load a track on each deck: title, art and waveform show on DECKS.
  • ☐ A small window beside the visuals? DECKS → LAYOUT → STACKED.
  • ☐ If you use them tonight: the SOUND IN meter moves, SHOW CONTROL rows say they're sending, the HARD STOP app sees the box.
  • ☐ Tap GHOST LINK at the top and show the crew the QR. Crew who send calls or need controls get tonight's crew code, not the password.

AFTER

  • ☐ The END OF NIGHT card shows after MUSIC OFF. SAVE IMAGE if you want it.
  • ☐ PLAYED: COPY TRACKLIST or PLAYLIST. Share the tracklist only if the DJ says yes.
  • ☐ Unplug the box. That's the normal off.
  • ☐ Write down anything odd while you remember it.

Something not right? Something's wrong, by what you see.
