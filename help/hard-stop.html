USING IT WITH HARD STOP

HARD STOP is the booth countdown panel from Third Polar Arch: a screen by the decks counting down to the cutoff, run from the HARD STOP app. GHOST LINK and HARD STOP are separate devices. They work fine apart, and better together.

NEEDS THE NEXT HARD STOP UPDATE

GHOST LINK is ready on its side. The parts below switch on with the next HARD STOP app and panel update.

HARD STOP TO GHOST LINK

Enter the night once, in HARD STOP. The app passes doors, first set, cutoff and the lineup to GHOST LINK over the venue Wi-Fi.

  • GHOST LINK uses the first set (or doors) as STARTS: music before it is SOUNDCHECK.
  • If you haven't planned the night in GHOST LINK, HARD STOP's lineup and cutoff become the plan, and each DJ's name switches on when their first track goes on air.
  • Anything you did plan in GHOST LINK wins. HARD STOP only fills the gaps.
  • GHOST LINK never changes anything in HARD STOP. PLAYED shows TIMES FROM HARD STOP when it's using them.
  • The room's level from HARD STOP's mic shows on STATUS > GEAR CARE for the crew (level only, nothing recorded), with a note after 20 minutes over 100 dB.

GHOST LINK TO HARD STOP

  • Now playing: title, artist, BPM and time left, for the panel. Off on the panel by default; never shown to the room unless the DJ turns it on.
  • MUSIC OFF, confirmed: after the cutoff, GHOST LINK tells HARD STOP when the music has really stopped.
  • Deck info for the crew: BPM, key, beat, phrase, time left and a small waveform per deck, for HARD STOP CONTROL panels (SPACESHIP) at lights, visuals and FOH.

GHOST LINK problems go to the crew, never to a DJ-facing alert mid-set. Both stay listen-only toward the decks and the sound.

SETTING IT UP

Put the box on the venue Wi-Fi (how) and keep the phone running the HARD STOP app on the same Wi-Fi. The app finds GHOST LINK by itself. Nothing to pair.
