HELP / KNOWN ISSUES

KNOWN ISSUES

What doesn't work as well as it should yet, said plainly. An issue comes off this list when its fix ships.

Updated 8 November 2026.

WITHOUT A MIXER, PLAYED GUESSES

With no mixer on the network, nothing says which deck is out loud. PLAYED counts the MASTER deck after 30 seconds, which is right most of the time but can miss a short blend or count a long preview. Cable the mixer to the switch for the real thing.

XDJ-XZ: LOOPS SHOW AFTER THE FIRST TIME ROUND

XZ-style players say they're looping but don't send where the loop is. GHOST LINK works it out from the first jump back, so the loop shows on the waveform after it has gone round once.

A "ONE MORE" AFTER A CHANGEOVER CAN MERGE SETS

If the outgoing DJ plays one more track from their USB after the next DJ has started, GHOST LINK reads it as a backup stick and joins the two sets. Fix it with SET or USBS TONIGHT in PLAYED.

SONG SECTIONS NEED PHRASE-ANALYSED TRACKS ON RECENT PLAYERS

Intro / up / chorus / down and the chorus countdown come from rekordbox phrase analysis. The track must be analysed for phrases, and the player must share it. The XDJ-XZ doesn't. Without it, those blocks stay empty; everything else works.

SLIP AND EMERGENCY LOOP AREN'T SHOWN

The players don't send them on the network.

THE CLOCK CAN BE OFF UNTIL A PHONE CONNECTS

Only on a box without a clock battery. With no internet it sets its time from the first phone that opens the dashboard. Until then BOOTH CHECK says CLOCK NOT CONFIRMED and early times in the logs can be off.

GUEST WI-FI THAT KEEPS DEVICES APART

Some guest Wi-Fi stops devices seeing each other, so phones can't reach the box. Use the GHOST LINK Wi-Fi there.

GHOSTLINK.LOCAL DOESN'T OPEN ON SOME PHONES

Use the number address from the share panel, gl.cold.fyi, or 10.42.0.1 on the GHOST LINK Wi-Fi.

IPHONES CAN'T VIBRATE FOR ALERTS

A web page can't make an iPhone vibrate. Android phones do.
