GHOST LINK - FOR DJS
There's a GHOST LINK box in the booth tonight. Here's what it means for you.

WHAT IT IS
A small box next to the decks, with one cable into the DJ network switch. It reads what's on your USB as it plays: title, artist, BPM, key, time left, waveform. The crew watches that on their phones.

WHAT YOU'LL SEE
Maybe nothing. Maybe a phone or a screen in the booth with the decks on it. You can open it too: the other deck's time left, key and BPM at a glance.

WHAT TO DO
Nothing. Play like you always do. If you share a USB or play B2B, tell the booth whose USB is whose so the sets get the right names.

YOUR TRACKLIST
It's logged for the crew, so they can see what went out. It only goes public if you say so. Off by default. If the booth has a sound card, a set is only recorded when someone taps RECORD for it: say if you'd rather not.

NOT TO WORRY ABOUT
It never touches the mixer or the sound, and leaves the decks alone unless the box's owner turns on its deck controls. It's listen-only on the DJ network, and nothing passes through it. It doesn't copy your music. If it stops, the booth doesn't notice. Playing from a laptop? Then it just won't see those decks; nothing to change.

More: https://ghostlink.cold.fyi/help/advance/for-djs.html
