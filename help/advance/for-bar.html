WHAT IT IS

GHOST LINK: a small box by the decks that shows what's playing on the crew's phones.

IF YOU WANT

Ask the booth for the link. NOW → MANAGER shows who's on, when the set ends and when the music stops.

NOT TO WORRY ABOUT

It doesn't control the music. Unplugging it doesn't stop the music; it's safe to unplug, but leave it be if you can.
