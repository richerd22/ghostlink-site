GHOST LINK - FOR LIGHTS, VISUALS AND SOUND
Live deck data on any phone, from a box that never touches the mixer or the sound.

WHAT IT IS
A small box on the DJ network that reads every deck and shows it on any phone browser. No app.

LIGHTS AND VISUALS
BPM, the beat (bar one marked), the song section and what's next, and scrolling waveforms. Open the link, tap NOW, then LIGHTS or VJ. For software (Resolume, TouchDesigner, MadMapper, QLC+, lighting desks) it can send beat, downbeat, BPM, section and the on-air track as OSC over Wi-Fi: ask the box's owner to add your computer.

SOUND AND MANAGERS
Alerts on every phone at once: DEAD AIR, a deck ending with nothing loaded, a USB pulled from a playing deck. NOW > TECH or MANAGER for network health, set times and who's on. STATUS has a one-glance BOOTH CHECK.

WHAT TO DO
Join the Wi-Fi you're told (the venue's, or one called GHOST LINK). Open the link. If you need booth controls, ask for tonight's 4-digit crew code.

NOT TO WORRY ABOUT
It never sends anything to the mixer or the sound, and nothing to the decks unless the box's owner turns on its deck controls. It's listen-only on the DJ network, and nothing passes through it. Song sections need phrase-analysed tracks on recent players.

More: https://ghostlink.cold.fyi/help/advance/for-crew.html
