WHAT IT IS

A small box in the booth that reads the decks and shows them on any phone browser. No app.

WHAT YOU'LL SEE

Open the link the venue sends you, tap NOW, then PHOTO: a big countdown to the next chorus, the section you're in, and time left in the track.

WHAT TO DO

Join the Wi-Fi they tell you (the venue's, or one called GHOST LINK). Open the link. Keep the screen awake from the HELP tab.

GOOD TO KNOW

The countdown needs tracks analysed for phrases and a recent player; not every track or player has it. Time left always works.

NOT TO WORRY ABOUT

You can't change anything by accident: watching is open, controls are locked. It never touches the sound, and leaves the decks alone unless the owner turns on deck controls.

Print this page for the booth: it prints black on white.
