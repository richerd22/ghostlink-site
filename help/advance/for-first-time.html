WHAT IT IS

GHOST LINK reads the DJ decks over the DJ network and shows them on phones: track, BPM, key, time left, waveforms. It keeps a list of what played tonight, for the crew.

WHAT IT DOESN'T DO

It never touches the mixer or the sound, and leaves the decks alone unless the box's owner turns on its deck controls. It's listen-only, and nothing passes through it. It doesn't copy anyone's music.

WHAT TO DO

Nothing. If you're crew and want to watch, ask the booth for the link or the QR code.

IF IT'S IN THE WAY

It's safe to unplug; the music won't notice. Ask whoever brought it first.
