THE SHORT VERSION, FOR ANYONE

  • It reads what's on the DJ's USB as it plays: title, artist, BPM, key.
  • The tracklist stays with the crew. Going public is the DJ's call, off by default.
  • Lights and visuals get BPM, beat, phrase and waveform. Sound and managers get dead-air and gear alerts.
  • Nothing touches the decks, the mixer or the sound. It's listen-only on the DJ network.
