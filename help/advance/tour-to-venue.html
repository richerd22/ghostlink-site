GHOST LINK - FOR VENUES
What GHOST LINK needs from your booth, and what it doesn't.

WHAT IT IS
A small box (no fan, no screen) that plugs into your DJ network and shows the decks on phones: track, BPM, time left, waveforms, and a log of what played. It only listens.

WHAT WE NEED
One free port on the DJ network switch, or the DJ router the players plug into. One power outlet in the booth. Optional: your Wi-Fi name and password, so the crew's phones stay on your Wi-Fi.

NO FREE PORT?
We bring a small plain switch. It sits between the router and one cable. Please don't have it plugged straight into one player: then it only sees that player.

NO WI-FI?
Fine. It makes its own Wi-Fi called GHOST LINK for the crew. No internet needed.

NOT TO WORRY ABOUT
It never sends anything to the mixer or the sound, and nothing to the decks unless the box's owner turns on its deck controls. Nothing passes through it; if it dies, the booth carries on. Nothing to install, nothing on your network changes, and your Wi-Fi password stays on the box. The DJ's tracklist stays with the crew; it goes public only if the DJ says so.

GOOD TO KNOW
Guest Wi-Fi that keeps devices apart won't work for it; then it uses its own Wi-Fi.

More: https://ghostlink.cold.fyi/help/advance/for-venues.html
