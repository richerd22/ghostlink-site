THE SCREEN

The dashboard is one page with six tabs across the top: NOW, DECKS, NETWORK, PLAYED, STATUS and HELP. Every phone on the same Wi-Fi as the box can open it, in the browser. Anything you set up for looks (layout, role, text size) is saved on that phone only.

The ghost at the top left shows how the box is doing. GHOST LINK next to it is a button: tap it to share the dashboard.

DECKS

One tile per player:

  • Title, artist, artwork and the player's model.
  • BPM (with the pitch), pitch %, time left, ON AIR in red (from the mixer) and MASTER.
  • A scrolling colour waveform with the playhead, and the whole track below it with the cue points. Tap a waveform to zoom.
  • Tags when they apply: KEY, LOOP (with its length, shaded on the waveform), SYNC, REVERSE, CUED, END.
  • A beat counter that flashes on the beat, and song sections (intro, up, chorus, down) when the track and the player have them.
  • PLAYED 23:14 in amber: this track was already on air tonight. LAST PLAYED: it was played on a past night. PLAYED HERE: played at this venue before (amber when it's recent).
  • MIX 8A→9A ✓ +2.1% on a cued deck: whether its key fits what's on air, and the tempo change it needs.
  • LAST SEEN: the box isn't reading right now. The tile keeps the last track and stops the clock instead of guessing.

A deck stuck on LOADING: the box retries by itself. After 20 seconds a RESTART READING button shows on the tile too. See restart reading a deck.

NOW

One big readout of what the room is hearing. Pick who you are at the top: DJ, PHOTO, VJ, LIGHTS, SOUND, TECH, MANAGER or HOST. Each is only a starting layout; nobody is locked out of anything.

DJ
Track, ON NOW (your set: 30 MIN LEFT · NEXT and who, music off), time left, BPM, key, the other deck, section, all decks.
PHOTO
A big countdown to the next chorus (and how many bars), the section, time left.
VJ
Track, BPM, beat counter, section, next section with bars to go, DROP IN (bars to the chorus), show control, key.
LIGHTS
BPM, big beat squares, section and the next one, bars to the drop, SHOW CONTROL (which outputs are on and sending).
SOUND
For the sound tech: the SOUND IN level and peak, silence, the room's level when HARD STOP's mic sends it, track, set times, the box.
TECH
THE BOX (temperature, Wi-Fi, players, sound in, recording, uptime), network health, all decks, set times.
MANAGER
On now, the night in numbers (tracks, DJs, since, peak BPM, with a link to the recap), the lineup, all decks.
HOST
For the door or host: who's on and who's next, the lineup, music off.

EDIT shows, hides and reorders the blocks. Saved on that phone. To send someone straight to a role, add ?role=photo (or any role) to the address. Set times come from tonight's plan, HARD STOP, or the venue's usual times.

LAYOUTS

DECKS → LAYOUT. Start from a preset: CLASSIC, BIG WAVES, STACKED, WAVES ONLY or COMPACT.

  • STACKED puts every deck's scrolling waveform in one block with the playheads lined up, so you can see two tracks against each other.
  • WAVES ONLY is just the waveforms, big.

Then change any part: decks across, waveform height (S to GIANT), zoom (4, 8, 16 or 32 seconds across), deck text size, what shows, waveform colour (FULL COLOUR, BLUE, WHITE), REMAINING or ELAPSED, dim the decks that aren't on air, which decks to show.

Small window (in a corner of the screen beside Resolume or Quickshow): the header shrinks to one line and warnings to one line (tap to read all of it), so the decks get the room. STACKED works best there: every deck fits without scrolling. Each waveform gets one line with the deck's state, BPM, pitch, key and time left; the decks you can hear get the most room, MIXING shows when two are out loud, NEXT marks the deck cued up next, and an empty deck is a thin line. Want the full deck tiles back underneath? LAYOUT → DECK TILES BELOW.

COPY LINK TO THIS LAYOUT makes a link that opens the same layout on another phone or a booth screen.

ALERTS

Alerts are worked out on the box, so every open phone shows the same thing at the same time.

DEAD AIR
Music was going out, and nothing has been on air for 10 seconds.
ENDS IN 20s
The only deck going out ends in under 30 seconds and nothing is loaded on another deck.
USB OUT
The USB or SD card a playing track comes from is being pulled. The track keeps going; plug it back before loading the next one.
LOST CONNECTION
This phone lost the box. It reconnects by itself. Check the phone is on the same Wi-Fi.

Android phones vibrate for dead air and USB out. iPhones can't vibrate from a web page.

A warning you've seen can be put away on that phone with OK, NOT TONIGHT. It comes back the next night. If a phone was away for a while, it shows WHILE YOU WERE AWAY with what happened meanwhile.

PLAYED

The night's tracklist. A track counts once it has been on air for 10 seconds. Cue previews don't count.

No mixer on the DJ network? PLAYED still works: it guesses from the MASTER deck, and a track counts after 30 seconds. See no mixer.

Under the list: recap numbers, BPM across the night, a timeline, and THE NIGHT IN NUMBERS (pace, tempo, how the DJs mixed: cuts or long blends and how long, mixes in key, the night's energy, loops, genres, artists, who played what). Pick a past night from the menu at the top.

TRANSITIONS rates how well the beats sat together while two decks or more were out loud: clean, a flam, or a gallop. BOX RECORDS keeps this box's records (fastest track, longest blend, biggest pitch, most decks at once and more) until they're beaten. HALL OF FAME shows the records, the cleanest blends and the WILD RIDES (add ?tv=1 for a TV). Names on WILD RIDES stay hidden unless the owner turns them on.

A picture of the night, ready to post.
COPY SETLIST
The list as plain text.
COPY TRACKLIST

PLAYLIST

RECAP IMAGE is next to the night menu; the rest are under COPY · SAVE. Step by step: get the tracklist or playlist.

SETS, STARTS AND SOUNDCHECK

ALL NIGHTS (top of the night list in PLAYED): every night on the box. Pick a venue or a DJ, or search a track or artist: most played, who played what where, genres, keys, and when the music peaks. Tap a night to open it. Pick a DJ to see their profile: how they mix, their tempo, their most played tracks, night by night. See look back at every night.

ON THE DECKS in PLAYED shows whose set it is. Tracks land under that name.

  • By hand: type the name and tap SET.
  • With a planned night: it switches by itself. The next DJ takes over when their first track goes on air from a new USB, or 15 minutes after their start time if no new USB shows (same USB, B2B, laptop).
  • With no plan: when a USB that hasn't been on air tonight goes on air, a new set starts: SET 2, SET 3... Say whose it is with SET. If the USB's own name looks like a person's name, a one-tap suggestion shows next to SET.
  • Backup stick or B2B: if the previous DJ's USB comes back within 2 tracks, the split is undone by itself.

STARTS: the time a night begins. Music on air before it is SOUNDCHECK, kept in its own block in PLAYED and in COPY TRACKLIST. Set it when you plan a night. With no time set, the night starts at 9 PM (or 20 minutes before the first set, if that's earlier).

USBS TONIGHT

PLAYED → USBS TONIGHT (folded, under ON THE DECKS) lists each USB that went on air tonight. Say whose it is once and SAVE. The next time that USB starts a set, on any night, GHOST LINK names the set by itself. Naming the USB that started the current set names that set too.

Step by step: name a set or say whose USB.

SHARING THE TRACKLIST

The tracklist is the DJ's. PLAYED shows on phones that open the dashboard on the box's Wi-Fi, which is the crew in the room. On WATCH FROM HOME (the link that works from anywhere), the decks are live but PLAYED stays hidden until someone at the venue taps TRACKLIST: CREW ONLY so it says TRACKLIST: SHARED. Only do that when the DJ says yes. It goes back to crew only the next night.

Step by step: share the tracklist.

NETWORK

Everything on the DJ network: each player and mixer by number, and GHOST LINK on its own number (7 to 15, never a player's). EVENTS is a list of what happened, in plain English.

SOUND IN (on NETWORK): plug a small USB sound card into the box and the mixer's REC OUT into the card. You get a level meter, a SILENCE warning when the room goes quiet, and SET RECORDINGS: nothing records unless you tap ● RECORD for that set (crew password). It ends when the next DJ is on, and anyone can tap STOP RECORDING. Each set gets its TRACKLIST and a lossless download. A red ● REC shows while it records. See record a set and which card.

STATUS

BOOTH CHECK at the top is the one-glance check before doors: CLOCK, CLOCK BATTERY, VENUE, WI-FI, DJ CABLE, JOINED, PLAYERS, MIXER, TRACKS, DECK SETUP, POWER, BLACK BOX. The words say if something needs a look; colour is only a hint.

VENUE asks (YOUR VENUE? · not checked tonight) until tonight's venue is confirmed: picked by hand, set by tonight's plan, or found by joining that venue's saved Wi-Fi. It keeps PLAYED from being filed under last night's room.

WHO'S HERE lists what's watching or nearby: HARD STOP panels and the HARD STOP app, phones, tablets and computers. Decks and mixers are on NETWORK.

CLOCK BATTERY shows when the box has one: a small coin cell that keeps the time while it's unplugged, so the clock is right even with no internet. GOOD is fine. LOW or FLAT: swap in a new CR2032. A rechargeable ML2032 also works, but the box only charges it once charging is switched on for it; it stays off for regular coin cells, which must never be charged.

GEAR CARE · FYI shows up under the problems when something could be hard on the gear: a low-quality file (160 kbps or less) on air and, if the mixer's USB goes to the box with its controls named, a trim near the top, an EQ boosted all the way, or the master or booth near max. Each one says what to do about it. These are for the tech, never an alarm, and the box can't hear the sound itself: the red light on the mixer is the truth. With HARD STOP at the booth, its mic adds the room's level (ROOM ~98 dB) and a note after 20 minutes over 100 dB.

Below it, any problems, each with the cause and the fix in plain words (for example Two players are both number 2, No mixer on the DJ network, Power low).

GO TO at the very top finds any setting, switch or section on any tab: type wifi, record or rips and tap the answer. Each answer says where it lives; it opens right there and blinks. On a laptop, / jumps to it and 1-6 switch tabs. The page opens on the tab you used last.

Everything else sits in four groups, each line folded until you open it. A small ? next to a note shows the longer version.

TONIGHT
NIGHTS, DECK CONTROLS, SONG ID FOR THE CROWD.
THIS VENUE
VENUE (with MUSIC AT THIS VENUE), WI-FI, ROOMS.
SCREENS & OUTPUTS
SHOW CONTROL, OSC OUT, SCREENS (TV MODE, STREAM OVERLAY, SONG PAGE, PRINT STICKER), WATCH FROM HOME.
THE BOX
CONTROLS, BACKUP (with BLACK BOX and LOGS), UPDATES, BOX DETAILS, TOOLS.

What each one does:

VENUE
Name, players expected, address, rooms, gear, contacts, booth notes and the venue Wi-Fi.
MUSIC AT THIS VENUE
Under VENUE: the room's usual start and MUSIC OFF (used on any night you didn't plan), its usual BPM, how many days a repeat counts as recent, a NOT HERE list (a title, an artist, or artist - title) and the room's music in words. Decks show PLAYED HERE, NOT HERE and SLOWER / FASTER THAN HERE. They're notes: GHOST LINK never stops a DJ for them. See set a venue's music notes.
NIGHTS
PLAN A NIGHT: date, venue, STARTS, MUSIC OFF, lineup, notes.
WI-FI
AUTO, BE THE HOTSPOT or KNOWN WI-FI ONLY.
CONTROLS
REJOIN, RESTART READING, LISTEN ONLY, ALLOW JOIN, RESTART, SHUT DOWN. RESTART and SHUT DOWN: tap, then hold for a second.
SCREENS
TV MODE, STREAM OVERLAY, SONG PAGE, PRINT STICKER.
BACKUP
BACKUP keeps venues, nights, played lists and the password in one file. RESTORE puts it back. BLACK BOX and LOGS are here too.
UPDATES
Owner only. CHECK looks online, then INSTALL (hold) or WHEN THE MUSIC STOPS. No internet: get the update file from GHOST LINK support onto your phone, then INSTALL FROM FILE. GO BACK TO PREVIOUS puts the last version back. Never installs during music; a new version that doesn't start properly is undone by itself.
BOX DETAILS
Link, cable, players, Wi-Fi, uptime, temperature.
WATCH FROM HOME
A view-only link that works from anywhere.
TOOLS
DEMO, SHOW THE TOUR, BOO'S ARCADE, TEST RUN. The ghost switches are in HELP → DISPLAY.
BOO'S ARCADE
Under TOOLS: short games for anyone in the room. On the decks: RIDE THE WAVE (the ghost surfs the playing track; tap to jump, on the beat for a combo; the first ride is a short lesson), ON THE ONE (tap along to the room's beat), EQ KILL (kill the bass, bring it back on the drop) and BEATMATCH (line up two beat grids). Quick ones: CATCH THE DROP, TAP THE TEMPO (keep the beat going in the dark), CAMELOT (which key mixes in?) and DAILY RIDE (one course a day, the same for everyone). With tonight's tracks: HIGHER OR LOWER (faster or slower?) and SET LIST (in the order they played). CALIBRATE once per phone so the timing games allow for its delay. High scores stay on the box for every phone: all time, tonight and at this venue. TONIGHT ON A SCREEN shows the night's best with a code to scan, for a TV by the bar.

WI-FI

The box has one Wi-Fi radio. It's either on a Wi-Fi it knows, or it is the Wi-Fi (called GHOST LINK). Never both. Pick in STATUS → WI-FI:

AUTO
The default. Joins the strongest Wi-Fi it knows. Becomes the GHOST LINK Wi-Fi only when none is around (90 seconds at power-on, or 10 minutes after losing one), and keeps looking every 3 minutes.
BE THE HOTSPOT
Always the GHOST LINK Wi-Fi. The dashboard is at 10.42.0.1. No internet on it.
KNOWN WI-FI ONLY
Never the hotspot. Keeps trying the Wi-Fi it knows.

The box learns a Wi-Fi when you save it on a venue: add a venue's Wi-Fi. If one keeps refusing, it tries the next on its list, and resets its own Wi-Fi after 3 failed tries.

Guest Wi-Fi that keeps devices apart from each other won't work: phones can't reach the box. Use the GHOST LINK Wi-Fi then.

PASSWORD AND CREW CODE

Watching is open to anyone on the Wi-Fi. Changing things needs the password, once per phone (it stays unlocked for 30 days). The password your box starts with is on the sticker. Change it with CHANGE PASSWORD (8 or more characters; every phone has to unlock again).

LISTEN ONLY never needs the password: the safe button must never wait on typing.

CREW CODE: a new 4-digit code each night. It unlocks the booth controls on a phone until 10:00 the next morning, so you don't have to hand out the password. It can't change the password, backups, the box's name or venue Wi-Fi passwords. See give the crew code.

5 wrong tries from one phone means waiting a minute. LOCK locks this phone again.

VENUES AND NIGHTS

VENUE keeps what you need for a room: address, rooms, gear, contacts, booth notes, how many players to expect, and its Wi-Fi. Wi-Fi passwords stay on the box and only show on unlocked phones.

NIGHTS: plan a date, a venue, STARTS, MUSIC OFF and the lineup, one act per line (22:00 OPENER). A line without a time is fine too: a night's name, or one artist all night. Times before noon belong to the same night. On the night, the box picks the venue and switches ON THE DECKS by itself. A SET by hand wins until the next slot. See plan a night.

SHARE THE DASHBOARD

Tap GHOST LINK at the top: the address on this Wi-Fi, a QR code and COPY. On the GHOST LINK Wi-Fi the address is always 10.42.0.1. On the venue Wi-Fi, ghostlink.local works on most phones; if it doesn't, use the number address from the share panel. STATUS → SCREENS → PRINT STICKER prints QR codes for the booth.

SHORT LINKS

Each box has a short link: gl.cold.fyi/<name> (the name starts as ghostlink). Add a role for a starting view: gl.cold.fyi/<name>/photo, /vj, /lights, /manager.

  • It works on the same Wi-Fi as the box, when that Wi-Fi has internet.
  • From anywhere else, it works while WATCH FROM HOME is on.
  • On the GHOST LINK Wi-Fi there's no internet: use 10.42.0.1.

Change the name: tap GHOST LINK → CHANGE NAME → type it → SAVE. Needs the owner password (not the crew code). The new link works within a minute.

WATCH FROM HOME

A view-only link anyone can open from anywhere: the decks, live. No controls, and no PLAYED unless the tracklist is shared. STATUS → WATCH FROM HOME → TURN ON, then COPY LINK and send it. The box must be on a Wi-Fi with internet (not its own hotspot). It turns off after every restart, and TURN OFF ends it.

THE GHOST

The ghost at the top shows how the box is doing:

_ _ ASLEEP
No decks found.
. . LISTENING
Looking at the network before it joins.
o o LINKED
Reading the decks.
AMBER
Backed off: another device wanted its number. It comes back on a free one by itself, in about 30 seconds.

While music plays, a little copy of him goes and sits on the MASTER deck, under its waveform, never on it. Drag him anywhere to park him there; he stays put, even after a reload (handy in a small window). Drop him back on the big one to send him to the decks again. Tap him for a boop. He reacts to the room (a nod on the downbeat, faces at drops, loops and long blends) and never talks. He never covers the numbers.

All per phone, in HELP → DISPLAY (and STATUS → TOOLS): GHOST MOODS: ON, GHOST: GOES TO THE DECKS (or GHOST: STAYS UP TOP), GHOST SIZE: M (S, M, L, XL), and GHOST SMELLS RIPS: OFF: switch it on and he pulls a face at a track that looks ripped (free-download tags, rip-site names, low bitrate). It's about the file, never the DJ.

BLACK BOX

The box writes a plain-text log of the night as it goes: when it started, every deck change, players found and lost, Wi-Fi, power and problems. It survives a pulled plug. It stays on the box; nothing is sent anywhere.

STATUS → BACKUP → BLACK BOX downloads it as a zip, with the played lists (needs unlocking). See download the black box.

POWER

Just unplug it. The system is read-only and saves as it goes, so pulling the power can't hurt it. Plug it back in and it comes back up by itself.

Prefer a clean stop? STATUS → CONTROLS → SHUT DOWN (tap, then hold for a second). The screen says SHUTTING DOWN, then SAFE TO UNPLUG. Or use the button: Press the small button on the box once. In about 10 seconds the green light goes out and only the red light stays on: it’s off and safe to unplug. Press it once more to turn it back on: the green light comes back, and the dashboard is back in about 30 seconds. Pressing it a few times in a row just turns it off and on again; nothing is lost. See turn it off.

If it restarted when nobody asked, STATUS says Restarted - back on for 10 minutes, with why (power pulled or a dip, a crash, or a freeze). Power low means it isn't getting enough power: use its own power supply, push the cable all the way in, keep it off switched power strips. Give it air: not on an amp, not in a closed bag.

EXTRAS

TV MODE
STATUS → SCREENS → TV MODE: a big-screen view for a booth TV, projector or tablet. Tap for full screen.
STREAM OVERLAY
A see-through now-playing card for OBS (Browser Source).
SONG ID
SONG ID FOR THE CROWD: a "what's playing" page and QR for the room. Off unless the DJ is OK with IDs going out.
OSC OUT
For lights and visuals software (Resolume, TouchDesigner, MadMapper, QLC+, lighting desks): beat, downbeat, BPM, section and the on-air track, sent over Wi-Fi to the computers you list. Never to the players.
SHOW CONTROL
STATUS → SHOW CONTROL sends the DJ's tempo and bar to the room's lights and visuals. Each one is off until the owner turns it on, and a dot fills in while it's sending. ABLETON LINK: Resolume, Ableton Live, Traktor and other apps with Link follow the DJ, with their "1" on the DJ's "1" (turn Link on in the app, same Wi-Fi). MIDI CLOCK: plug a USB MIDI interface into the box and its MIDI OUT into the lighting desk. RESOLUME, GRANDMA3, QLAB, MADMAPPER, CHAMSYS MAGICQ, MILLUMIN, SYNESTHESIA and TOUCHDESIGNER: tap SET UP, type the computer's address, tap TEST, then turn it on; each row says what to switch on in that app. Never sent to the decks or the mixer. See hook up lights and visuals; the MIDI interface is in gear to buy.
DECK CONTROLS
Off until the owner turns it on. Then STATUS → DECK CONTROLS can stop every deck, start a loaded deck, or find a track on any USB or SD in the booth and load it onto a deck: a mix before doors with no laptop. Every button: tap, then hold for a second. It asks first before playing over music that's on air, or replacing a loaded track, and never loads onto a playing deck. The deck plays through its own channel, so the fader has to be up. Stopping sends the deck back to its cue point. Each deck also has FROM CUE (back to its cue point and play), SYNC ON / SYNC OFF and MASTER; they ask first if the change could move the tempo of music on air. Scratching, jumping around a track, effects and the mixer can't be controlled from the network. Not every player obeys: see known issues.
AUTO-STOP
For rooms with a hard curfew. Under DECK CONTROLS, ARM FOR TONIGHT stops every deck at the night's MUSIC OFF time (set it in PLAN A NIGHT, or in HARD STOP). Every phone shows a countdown bar from 10 minutes out, with CANCEL AUTO-STOP. It only stops once, never late, and resets the next night. HARD STOP CAN STOP IT lets the HARD STOP app stop the music in the last 10 minutes too. See stop the music at curfew.
HOUSE SETTINGS
Our club standard for every deck (vinyl jog, ±10 tempo, quantize on, auto cue to memory, single play so a deck never starts the next track by itself, and more). Change any line, then SEND TO STOPPED DECKS between DJs. BOOTH CHECK → DECK SETUP shows a deck in the wrong jog mode, or on different firmware from the others. Decks don't report the rest of their settings, so sending is how you know.
DEMO
STATUS → TOOLS → DEMO: made-up tracks on empty players, to show someone how it works. Only on that phone. END DEMO at the top.
TEST RUN
STATUS → TOOLS → TEST RUN: checks that tick themselves as each part works. For a new venue.
DISPLAY
HELP tab: NIGHT, DIM BOOTH or DAYLIGHT looks, text size, keep the screen awake. Per phone.

IF SOMETHING'S WRONG

STATUS → LISTEN ONLY. The box goes quiet on the DJ network straight away, no password. REJOIN brings it back. Last resort: pull the power. Nothing breaks. More in FAQ and KNOWN ISSUES.
