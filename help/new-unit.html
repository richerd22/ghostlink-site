NEW UNIT

What the first day with a GHOST LINK box is like, from out of the box to the end of the first night.

WHAT YOU NEED

  • The GHOST LINK box. Small, no fan, no screen.
  • Its own power supply. Phone chargers and USB ports on power strips are often too weak; the box will say Power low.
  • A network cable long enough to reach the DJ switch or router.
  • Your phone.
  • Good to have: a small plain switch (unmanaged, 5 ports) for booths where the DJ router has no free port.
  • Extras, only if you want them: a USB sound card for set recordings, a MIDI interface for lighting desks. See gear to buy.

FIRST POWER-ON

  1. POWER IT

    Plug in the power. The green light blinks fast: it's looking for a Wi-Fi it knows. It doesn't know any yet.

  2. WAIT 90 SECONDS

    With no Wi-Fi it knows, it makes its own. The green light slows to a 1-second blink.

  3. JOIN GHOST LINK

    On your phone, join the Wi-Fi called GHOST LINK. The password is on the sticker. A page pops up with the box's status: tap GOT IT.

  4. YOU'RE IN

    The dashboard opens at 10.42.0.1. A short tour shows the first time; SKIP or DONE and it never comes back.

  5. UNLOCK AND CHANGE THE PASSWORD

    STATUS → type the default password from the sticker → UNLOCK. Then CHANGE PASSWORD to one of your own (8 or more characters).

JOINING THE NETWORK

The GHOST LINK Wi-Fi works anywhere, but phones on it have no internet. Better: teach the box the venue's Wi-Fi (or your own). Then it joins by itself every time, and everyone stays on their usual Wi-Fi.

Add a venue's Wi-Fi takes a minute. You can save your home Wi-Fi the same way: make a venue called HOME.

The box keeps a list. At each venue it joins the strongest one it knows; with none around, it becomes the GHOST LINK Wi-Fi again.

PLUGGING INTO THE DJ NETWORK

Players and the mixer talk to each other over network cables, through a switch or the venue's DJ router (Pioneer calls it PRO DJ LINK). GHOST LINK listens on the same network.

  • Plug into the switch or DJ router the players and mixer use. Any free port.
  • Never straight into one player. Then it only sees that one player.
  • Router or switch full? Bring a plain unmanaged switch: move one cable from the router into the switch, run a cable from the switch back to that router port, and plug GHOST LINK into the switch.
  • The mixer on the network too gives you ON AIR and a better PLAYED. Without it, things still work: see no mixer.
  • Players must be in their normal standalone (LINK) mode. In laptop or controller mode they don't talk on the network.
Nothing passes through GHOST LINK.

It's not inline. If it dies, the booth carries on and nobody notices. It never sends anything to the mixer or the sound, and nothing to the decks unless the box's owner turns on its deck controls.

THE FIRST NIGHT

  1. BEFORE DOORS

    Plug in, power, and open STATUS. Read BOOTH CHECK top to bottom. Load a track on each deck: TRACKS should say TITLES ARRIVING.

  2. RUN THE TEST

    Optional, at a new venue: STATUS → TOOLS → TEST RUN. Each line ticks itself as it works. Leave it open while you soundcheck.

  3. PLAN THE NIGHT

    Optional: STATUS → NIGHTS → PLAN A NIGHT with STARTS and the lineup. Tracks then land under the right DJ by themselves. How.

  4. SHARE IT

    Tap GHOST LINK at the top and show the QR to the crew. Give the crew code to anyone who needs the booth controls.

  5. DURING

    Nothing to do. Alerts show on every phone if something's up.

  6. AFTER

    PLAYED has the night: COPY TRACKLIST, PLAYLIST, RECAP IMAGE. Then unplug the box.
