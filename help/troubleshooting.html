HELP / SOMETHING'S WRONG

SOMETHING'S WRONG

Find what you see. Do what it says. GHOST LINK is never in the sound, so the music is fine either way.

ANYTHING WEIRD MID-SET?

STATUS → LISTEN ONLY. The box goes quiet on the DJ network at once. No password. REJOIN brings it back. Last resort: pull the power. Nothing breaks.

NO DECKS

DECKS says No players on the DJ network, or NETWORK says NO DJ LINK DATA ARRIVING.

  • The cable goes into the switch or DJ router the players use. Not into one player, not into the venue's office network.
  • Players in their normal (LINK) mode. In laptop or controller mode they don't talk on the network.
  • BOOTH CHECK says DJ CABLE UNPLUGGED? Push the cable in at both ends. It comes back in seconds.
  • DJ CABLE IN, NO ADDRESS YET: give it a minute.
  • Fewer players than you have: that player is off, its network cable is out, or it's in laptop mode.

DJ CABLE: WHAT IT SAYS

The box reads its own network port and says which of these it is, and for how long (READY FOR TONIGHT, NO DECKS YET, the top of the screen):

NO LINK LIGHT
Nothing at the other end of the cable is answering: the cable, or the router or switch port it goes to. Reseat it at GHOST LINK, try another cord, another port, or a player's LINK port. The light on the box's port should blink. Two cords into the same port and still nothing? It's that port: try another one.
LINK, NO DJ GEAR
The cable works but it goes to something that isn't the DJ network (the venue's own router, a switch the players aren't on). Use the router or switch the players use, or a player's LINK port. Players in LINK mode, not USB to a laptop.
DJ GEAR SEEN, NOT JOINED
The players are there; GHOST LINK hasn't joined yet (another device used its number, or it's still trying). It sorts itself out; STATUS → REJOIN if it stays.
JOINED, NO TRACK DETAILS
Joined, but a player hasn't sent the track yet. Tap RELOAD on the deck; check the player has its USB or SD in.

Four players and a router: with four players, every port on a small DJ router can be taken. GHOST LINK needs its own free port on that router or switch, or a player's LINK port. If there's no free port, a small switch on the router's last port gives you more.

With no DJ Link at all, the night still runs on the sound card: a night by ear.

ONLY BPM, NO TITLES

LISTEN ONLY is on, or the box stepped aside for another device (the ghost goes amber, BACKED OFF). It rejoins by itself in about 30 seconds. If not: STATUS → REJOIN.

WAVEFORM BLANK

  • Just loaded? It takes a moment.
  • The strip says why: waiting for the player, a streaming track (no waveform from the player), or the player stopped answering and the box is retrying. After 10 seconds it shows RELOAD: tap it to ask the player again.
  • Stuck on LOADING: the box tries again by itself. After 20 seconds the deck shows RESTART READING; tap it. Decks go blank for a few seconds, then read again. More.
  • Still blank: the track may not be analysed in rekordbox. Waveforms come from the analysis on the USB.
  • No sections (INTRO, CHORUS)? Those need tracks analysed for phrases, on a player that shares them. See known issues.

TWO PLAYERS, ONE NUMBER

On one of them: Menu / Utility → PLAYER No. → a free number.

PHONES CAN'T CONNECT

  • The phone must be on the same Wi-Fi as the box. Look at the green light: solid = the box is on a Wi-Fi it knows; slow blink = it is the GHOST LINK Wi-Fi, so join that (password on the sticker); fast blink = no Wi-Fi yet, wait 90 seconds.
  • On the GHOST LINK Wi-Fi, open 10.42.0.1. The phone may say there's no internet: stay connected anyway.
  • On the venue Wi-Fi: gl.cold.fyi finds the box. If ghostlink.local doesn't open, use the number address (tap GHOST LINK at the top on a phone that's in).
  • Some guest Wi-Fi keeps devices apart, so phones never see the box. Use the GHOST LINK Wi-Fi there.
  • The page lost the box: it reconnects by itself, and says which kind. This phone is offline: turn its Wi-Fi back on. GHOST LINK is restarting: back in about 20 seconds. The box isn't answering: check the phone didn't hop to another Wi-Fi, and that the box has power.

HARD STOP DOESN'T SEE THE BOX

  • The phone with the HARD STOP app must be on the same Wi-Fi as the box. If the box is on its own Wi-Fi, join GHOST LINK on that phone.
  • In the HARD STOP app, turn GHOST LINK on in its settings.
  • On an iPhone, the app needs local network access: iPhone Settings → HARD STOP → Local Network on.
  • Guest Wi-Fi that keeps devices apart blocks it too. Use the GHOST LINK Wi-Fi.
  • Check from GHOST LINK: STATUS → WHO'S HERE lists the HARD STOP app when it's connected.

More: using it with HARD STOP.

CREW CALLS DON'T SHOW

  • Sending one needs the crew code or the password. Crew code.
  • A TV opened with ?nocrew in its address hides them on purpose.
  • Calls from HARD STOP panels need the HARD STOP app connected (see above).

DEAD AIR OR ENDS IN 20s

These are real warnings: nothing has been on air for 10 seconds, or the only deck playing is about to end with nothing loaded. They clear themselves when music is back.

LIGHTS OR VISUALS NOT FOLLOWING

STATUS → SHOW CONTROL → that row's TEST, and read its help line. Ableton Link: turn Link on in the app, on the same Wi-Fi as the box. More.

SOUND IN METER FLAT

The cable goes from the mixer's output (REC OUT, or MASTER 2 or BOOTH when REC OUT is taken: which one) to the sound card's INPUT (not output). No sound card: unplug it, plug it back, wait 5 seconds. More.

POWER LOW

Use the box's own power supply, cable all the way in. Phone chargers and power-strip USB ports are often too weak.

PLAYED ISN'T SAVING

The box's storage card is full or won't take writes. Nothing to do during the set: the list stays on screen, the black box keeps the last lines in memory, and both save by themselves when the card works again. Try not to pull the power until then. STATUS → BOX DETAILS → SELF-CHECK says how the box is doing.

Not here? Known issues lists what doesn't work as well as it should yet. Every change and problem is also in NETWORK → EVENTS.
