USING GHOST LINK WITH RESOLUME

Resolume Arena or Avenue 7 follows the DJ's tempo, with its bar on the DJ's bar, and can launch a column on the drop or on a new track.

WHAT IT GIVES RESOLUME

  • RESOLUME row: tempo, a resync on the DJ's downbeat, and (optional) a column on the drop, on a new track, at a crew call's GO or at music off.
  • Or ABLETON LINK instead, if you'd rather Resolume follow Link. Use one or the other for tempo, not both.
  • The dashboard in a small window beside Resolume: DECKS → LAYOUT → STACKED.

SET IT UP

  1. The Resolume computer on the same Wi-Fi as the box.

  2. In Resolume: Preferences → OSC → turn on OSC Input (port 7000).

  3. Unlock first, if this phone isn't already: STATUS → type the password (or tonight's crew code) where it says password or crew code → UNLOCK.

  4. STATUS → SHOW CONTROL → RESOLUME → SET UP: the computer's address, SAVE, TEST (Resolume's tempo jumps to the DJ's). Then TURN ON.

  5. Optional: type a column number for the drop or a new track. Blank = nothing.

CLIPS THAT FOLLOW THE DECKS (VISUALS LINK)

Under the RESOLUME row, VISUALS LINK launches clips for you. Both parts are off until you turn them on.

  1. Type the Resolume computer's address and tap RESOLUME BASIC: deck 1 plays on layer 1, deck 2 on layer 2, and clip 1 is the look when nothing else matches.

  2. Add rules if you like: TITLE HAS, ARTIST HAS, GENRE HAS or COMMENT TAG (a word you put in the track's comment in rekordbox, like #vj:intro), and the clip to launch. TEST each one.

  3. Turn on TRACK → CLIP. A clip launches when its track goes on air: the fader up on the mixer. A track waiting in headphones launches nothing.

  4. Optional: tick CLEAR A DECK'S LAYER WHEN IT GOES OFF AIR and the layer goes dark 2 seconds after that deck's fader comes down (a quick cut or scratch doesn't count). Bring the fader back up and the clip comes back. Tick AGAIN on a rule to launch its clip each time the deck comes back on air, not just once per track.

  5. Optional: PHRASE CUES send any Resolume address (a column, say) on bar 1 of the chorus, or 8 bars before the outro. They need tracks analysed with phrases in rekordbox, on players that share them; the XDJ-XZ doesn't, so there the cues just wait.

TURN IT OFF

STATUS → SHOW CONTROL → tap the row's ON so it says OFF. One tap; nothing more is sent. Or turn off OSC Input in Resolume.

GHOST LINK never opens, edits or saves your show file. It only sends live messages while a row is on; turn the row off and they stop. Nothing is ever sent to the decks or the mixer. In Resolume it only moves the tempo and, if you typed one, launches that column (the same as clicking it).

Tried on Arena 7.26. Avenue takes the same messages; not tried on Avenue yet.
