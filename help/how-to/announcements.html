HELP / HOW TO

PLAY ANNOUNCEMENTS FROM THE BOX

Play announcements (LAST CALL, LIGHTS ON IN 10, COAT CHECK CLOSES AT 2), sound effects and test sounds from a phone, from the SOUNDBOARD. The sound comes out of a small USB sound card on the box, into a spare channel on the mixer. Nothing plays unless the owner turned it on and someone presses a button.

It's for the night's housekeeping and a bit of fun. GHOST LINK is not a fire alarm or evacuation system. The venue's alarm and PA come first.

THE GEAR

This is the venue's own purchase, from any music shop. We don't sell it.

USB SOUND CARD
A Behringer UCA222 or UCA202, or any small USB card that needs no driver and has an RCA output. The same card also does SOUND IN: its INPUT listens to the mixer while its OUTPUT plays announcements.
INTO A LINE CHANNEL
A stereo RCA to RCA cable, male to male, long enough to reach the mixer.
INTO AN AUX OR BOOTH INPUT
RCA to 1/4-inch (6.35 mm) cables, if that input takes jacks. Check the back of the desk.

WIRING

  1. THE CARD INTO THE BOX

    Any USB port on the box. It's found within 5 seconds.

  2. THE CARD'S OUTPUT INTO A SPARE CHANNEL

    From the card's OUTPUT (not INPUT) to a spare LINE channel on the DJ mixer, or an AUX / booth input on the house desk. Red is right, white is left.

    Never into the master path (not the master insert, not between the mixer and the amps). On its own channel, the fader is the volume: the DJ and FOH stay in charge and can pull it down any time.

  3. TURN IT ON

    On the dashboard: STATUS → TONIGHT → ANNOUNCEMENTS → TURN ON. It needs the owner's password (tonight's crew code can't). It stays on until the owner turns it off. ANNOUNCEMENTS and the SOUNDBOARD only show while the sound card is plugged in; pull it out and they step aside, with your settings and sounds kept.

LEVEL CHECK BEFORE DOORS

  1. FADER DOWN

    The channel (or AUX) the card is on: fader all the way down.

  2. 1 kHz TONE

    SOUNDBOARD → TEST → 1 kHz TONE, hold PLAY for 1 second. A steady tone for 3 seconds at a sensible line level.

  3. OR A CHIME

    Five sounds are built in and always there, as pads on the SFX tab: TPA CHIME, TPA CHIME LONG, TPA NOTE, and the THIRD POLAR ARCH and TPA tags. A chime is a friendlier level check than the tone, and a good way to get the room's attention before an announcement.

  4. BRING IT UP

    Raise the channel slowly while the tone plays, to where you want speech to sit. Then play one of your announcements to check it in the room.

THE SOUNDBOARD

Tap SOUNDBOARD in the dashboard's tabs (it shows while ANNOUNCEMENTS is on and the card is plugged in), or type "sfx" or "pa" in GO TO. Five tabs; the phone remembers the last one:

DROPS
Hype-man drops (see below).
TOP
The ones you actually use: whatever you pinned, then the most played sound effects and announcements on this box.
SFX
Big square pads. Hold one for 1 second to play it, or tap ARM and a single tap plays (ARM lasts until you close the browser tab). A new sound effect cuts the one playing, but never an announcement. Don't want the meme ones tonight? HIDE THE GOOFY ONES. SHOW ALL brings them back.
PA
Announcements by kind (WELCOME, CROWD, MEDICAL, DOORS & CAPACITY, TECH PAUSE, WEATHER, END OF NIGHT, SYSTEM TEST), each with its full script so you know exactly what the room will hear. Many have TAKE 1 and TAKE 2: two reads of the same words, pick the one you like. Hold PLAY 1 second.
TEST
Test signals for checking the sound system, each with what it's for (below).
MINE
Everything you added, and anything you built.

On a laptop: 1 to 9 play the first nine pads while armed, Esc stops.

PLAY ON THIS PHONE

No sound card? The SOUNDBOARD still works, playing on the phone or laptop you're holding instead of the PA. It says so at the top, plainly: PLAYS ON THIS PHONE, not the PA. Find it with GO TO ("soundboard", "sfx", "pa", "drops"), or from the line under NETWORK → SOUND IN. With a sound card plugged in, PLAY ON: PA / THIS PHONE picks where it plays (PA unless you change it). It still needs the password or tonight's crew code.

QUICK TIME CALL

At the top of the PA tab: pick what (THE BAR CLOSES IN, COAT CHECK CLOSES IN, THE MUSIC ENDS IN...), when (FIVE to THIRTY MINUTES), and if you like an opener (ATTENTION PLEASE) and a closer (THANK YOU). It shows the words, then hold PLAY for 1 second, or SAVE it as your own. The ready-made time calls sit just below as a compact list.

PRESETS

One tap at the top of the board sets it up for the night: ALL, CLEAN (no goofy ones, no drops), PA ONLY, FESTIVAL (crowd, care, weather and doors announcements first, no sound effects). SAVE AS PRESET keeps what you pinned and hid as your own named preset, to switch back to any night.

DROPS

Hype-man style drops in their own tab. They count as goofy: HIDE THE GOOFY ONES (or the CLEAN preset) puts them away.

MAKE IT YOURS

  • EDIT: rename a sound, give it one of six colours, pin it to TOP, hide it, choose its default take, or fix its script. Hidden sounds are never deleted: SHOW ALL is one tap.
  • BUILD ONE (PA tab): pick pieces in order (your own clips or any announcement), see the whole script, PREVIEW HERE on your phone, then save it with a name. It plays the pieces back to back as one announcement. Record short pieces of your own ("Attention please.", "fifteen minutes.", a place) and add them as PIECE: they show only in BUILD.

ADD A SOUND

+ ADD A SOUND is on every tab. Choose SFX, PA or PIECE, then:

  • PICK FILES: one or several at once (voice memos, mp3, wav). Each is named from its file name; rename it later with EDIT.
  • RECORD: shows only where the browser lets the page use the microphone. Easiest: record a voice memo in your phone's own recorder first.

The phone turns each one into the box's format (48 kHz, 16-bit WAV), trims the quiet at both ends and evens out the level. Up to 2 minutes each. There's no limit on how many: the board shows what you've used and what's free, and the box always keeps room for set recordings. Saving, deleting and playing need the password or tonight's crew code.

TEST SIGNALS

For checking a sound system. Before any of them: the channel fader all the way down, then bring it up slowly.

PINK NOISE
Even hiss with the same energy in every octave, 30 seconds. For matching speaker levels, checking every box makes sound, and EQ with an analyzer app on a phone.
SINE SWEEP
One tone gliding from 20 Hz up to 20 kHz in 10 seconds. Listen for rattles and buzzes: whatever buzzes on the way up is loose. Keep it moderate: the very low and very high ends are hard on speakers and ears.
85 Hz SUB TONE
A steady low tone, 20 seconds, for the subwoofers: check they work and are in phase (walk the room: it shouldn't vanish in one spot). Keep it low and short: long loud sub tones heat up the speakers.
1 kHz TONE
The classic line-up tone, 3 seconds. Bring the channel up until the mixer's meter sits where you want it.
POLARITY CLICKS
Short clicks, two a second for 10 seconds. Hold a polarity checker (or a phone app) at each speaker: they should all read the same. They work at low level.

EMERGENCY ANNOUNCEMENTS

GHOST LINK is not a fire alarm or evacuation system. The venue's alarm and PA come first. If the venue's staff ask you to help, the EMERGENCY announcements are last on the PA tab, outlined in red: hold PLAY for 2 seconds, then confirm. If DECK CONTROLS is on, the confirm also offers STOP THE DECKS FIRST.

HARD STOP AND THE PA

Both off until you turn them on, under ANNOUNCEMENTS → HARD STOP (the owner's password). Turning one on asks once, and says what HARD STOP could play. The HARD STOP app has its own switch for each, and nothing plays unless both are on.

  • AUTO CALLS: at HARD STOP's 10 MIN, 5 MIN and MUSIC OFF the box plays the end-of-night card for it ("Ten minutes of music left tonight" and so on), once each a night. Pick another card, or NOTHING, for each. LAST CALL plays only if HARD STOP sends a last call time and you pick a card for it.
  • LET HARD STOP CALL THE PA: HARD STOP's panels and app can play sounds from the soundboard, after a confirm there. Never EMERGENCY or test signals, one every 5 seconds at most.
  • The block shows the app's side too: on, off, or not heard from. The STOP bar says who started it: PA: LAST CALL · by MAIN BAR panel. Anyone can STOP.

PLAYING

  • One at a time. A new sound effect cuts the one playing; an announcement waits until the one playing ends or is stopped.
  • If a deck is playing on air, an announcement asks first. Sound effects don't: over the music is the point.
  • ■ STOP: anyone at the venue, no password. While something plays, a bar with STOP shows on every screen of the dashboard.
  • Every play and stop goes in the night's log, with who pressed it (the owner or the crew code) and which phone.

WHAT TO TELL THE DJ AND FOH

  • Which channel the box is on, and that it's the announcements channel.
  • It only makes a sound when someone holds a button on the dashboard. Nothing plays by itself.
  • Their fader is the volume. Down = silent, whatever the box does.

IF IT'S QUIET

NO SOUND CARD OUTPUT
The card's USB isn't in the box, or it's loose. Plug it in; the dashboard sees it within a few seconds.
PLAYS, BUT NOTHING IN THE ROOM
The cable is in the card's INPUT instead of OUTPUT, or the channel fader, its input selector (LINE) or the crossfader is in the way.
TOO LOUD OR TOO QUIET
Set it with the channel gain and fader during the 1 kHz TONE. The box has no volume knob of its own.
NO SOUNDBOARD BUTTON
It shows only while ANNOUNCEMENTS is on and the sound card is plugged in. Without a card, GO TO "soundboard" opens it to play on your phone.
