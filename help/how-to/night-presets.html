NIGHT PRESETS AND ALL SETTINGS

One tap sets GHOST LINK up for the kind of night. One list shows every setting. One file takes them to another unit.

NIGHT PRESETS

  1. Unlock with the owner's password: STATUS → type the password → UNLOCK.

  2. STATUS → SETTINGS · NIGHT PRESETS. Tap the kind of night.

  3. A list shows exactly what will change, like SONG ID FOR THE CROWD: ON → OFF. Nothing has changed yet. Tap APPLY, or CANCEL.

  4. Changed your mind? UNDO puts it all back, for 10 minutes.

  • CLUB NIGHT: decks on the DJ network, the usual night. LIGHTS pulse on the beat.
  • LAPTOP NIGHT: a laptop DJ on the sound card. GHOST LINK listens for the BPM and the drop, and your phone remembers the mixer as LAPTOP DJ.
  • HOME PRACTICE: kept as a practice night, quiet. Nothing announced, nothing sent to HARD STOP or the room. The LIGHTS look is OFF.
  • PRE-DOORS: soundcheck. The soundboard opens on PA and TEST, nothing goes out to the room yet. LIGHTS go CALM.
  • + SAVE AS PRESET: keeps how the box is set right now, under your own name.

A preset never turns on DECK CONTROLS, ANNOUNCEMENTS or LIGHTS: those stay a tap of yours, in their own section. Anything that would play on the PA only goes on when ANNOUNCEMENTS is already on, and the list says so in amber. A ✓ on a preset means the box is already set like that.

THE VENUE REMEMBERS

Use a preset with a venue picked and GHOST LINK remembers it for that venue. Next time the venue is tonight's, it asks once: USE CLUB NIGHT LIKE LAST TIME? with the list of what would change. NOT TONIGHT and it won't ask again tonight. It never changes anything by itself.

A planned night can name one too: NIGHTS → EDIT → PRESET. On the night it asks once, USE HOME PRACTICE?, the same way. If the night and the venue name different ones, the night's is the one it asks about.

Your own presets: YOUR PRESETS · RENAME OR DELETE under the preset buttons.

ALL SETTINGS

ALL SETTINGS lists every setting on the box in one place, with a search box. Each line says what it does and what it's on now. A dot means it's not the way it came; BACK TO DEFAULT puts it back. OPEN goes to its own section. THIS PHONE at the bottom is your phone's theme, text size and ghost. The crew code can look but not change.

MOVE SETTINGS TO ANOTHER UNIT

  1. On this box: SAVE SETTINGS FILE. Keep the file on your phone.

  2. On the other box: LOAD A SETTINGS FILE. The same list shows what will change, plus any venues and presets it adds. APPLY, and UNDO for 10 minutes.

The file has your settings, your presets and your venues. Never a password, the venues' Wi-Fi passwords, keys, tokens, share links or the box's id. Each box keeps its own password, name and Wi-Fi.
