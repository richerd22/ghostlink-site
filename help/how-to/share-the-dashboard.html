SHARE THE DASHBOARD

  1. Tap GHOST LINK at the top of the dashboard.

  2. Show the QR code, or tap COPY and send the address.

  3. Anyone on the same Wi-Fi opens it in their browser. No app.

The panel also shows the box's short link (gl.cold.fyi/<name>) and role links like /photo or /vj. Those need the Wi-Fi to have internet. On the GHOST LINK Wi-Fi, share 10.42.0.1 instead.

To rename the short link: CHANGE NAME in the same panel (owner password), then SAVE. For the booth wall: STATUS → PRINT STICKER.
