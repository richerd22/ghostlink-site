HELP / HOW TO

NIGHT SAFETY

For the moments a night needs everyone on the same page: stop the show, hold it for weather, know a crew call was seen, count down to MUSIC OFF, count the door.

GHOST LINK is not a fire alarm or an evacuation system. The venue's alarm and PA come first.

SHOW STOP

  1. NOW → SHOW STOP (needs tonight's crew code or the password).

  2. Pick STOP THE MUSIC, CLEAR THE FLOOR, MEDICAL or type your own, then SHOW IT ON EVERY SCREEN.

Every GHOST LINK screen goes red with the message: the dashboards, the TV, the stage clock, the crew screen. The stream overlay goes blank. If you use LIGHTS, they go steady white. The DJ stops the music: GHOST LINK never touches the players. It stays up, even through a restart, until someone taps ALL CLEAR.

Optional: pick a PA line for it under STATUS → NIGHT SAFETY. SHOW STOP then offers a tick to play it (with ANNOUNCEMENTS on).

HOLD

NOW → HOLD: WEATHER, LIGHTNING, STAGE FIX, SAFETY or your own words. Screens say HOLD: WEATHER, the stage clock stops the set's countdown, and MUSIC OFF stays where it is. LIGHTNING starts a 30 minute ALL CLEAR clock (tap it again after another strike). RESUME ends the hold, and the set gets its minutes back. Outdoors, WEATHER can ask HOLD? when gusts or thunder are due; a person still decides.

CREW CALLS: SEEN AND DONE

Every crew call on the dashboard has SEEN and DONE. Whoever sent it sees SEEN 23:41 (BOOTH). A call nobody has seen after a minute pulses once more on the screens (never a sound). Tick URGENT when you send one: it goes first, red and bigger. More about crew calls.

CURFEW LADDER

A list of steps before MUSIC OFF, each firing once a night. Off until the owner turns it on in STATUS → NIGHT SAFETY (only you turn it on: no preset ever does). Each step can put a line on the TV ("30 MIN TO GO"), send a crew call or a WRAP IT UP, play a spoken clip on the PA (only on a step you added one to, with ANNOUNCEMENTS on), send an OSC cue, or turn the lights a colour until MUSIC OFF. You set every step yourself: when, what it says, what it sends. TEST tries a step now. A PA clip never plays over another one or a crew call.

DOOR COUNT

Off until the owner turns it on and sets the capacity. The door phone opens door.html on the box (with the crew code) and taps + and −. The TV and stage clock show IN 412 / 450: yellow when it's nearly full, red when it's full. The night's peak goes on the END OF NIGHT card and the NIGHT REPORT.

HEARING CARD

Off until the owner turns it on. Every so often the TV shows a small calm card for 15 seconds: EARPLUGS AT THE BAR · LOOK AFTER YOUR EARS, or your own words.

With the HARD STOP app, its hold, door count and cutoff show here too, and its panels can mark crew calls SEEN. Its panels never start SHOW STOP or a PA line.
