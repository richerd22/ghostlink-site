HOOK UP LIGHTS AND VISUALS

Send the DJ's tempo and bar to Resolume, a lighting desk, QLab or any app with Ableton Link. Each one is off until you turn it on, and nothing is ever sent to the decks or the mixer.

  1. Put the lights or visuals computer on the same Wi-Fi as the box.

  2. Unlock first, if this phone isn't already: STATUS → type the password (or tonight's crew code) where it says password or crew code → UNLOCK.

  3. Open STATUS → SHOW CONTROL. Pick a row (below).

  4. For the app rows: type the computer's address, SAVE, then TEST. Then TURN ON.

  5. The dot on the row fills in while it's sending.

ABLETON LINK
For Resolume, Ableton Live, Traktor and other apps with Link. Turn Link on in the app too. It follows the DJ's tempo, with its "1" on the DJ's "1". Nothing to type.
MIDI CLOCK
For a lighting desk or drum machine with a 5-pin MIDI IN. Needs a USB MIDI interface in the box; its MIDI OUT to the desk's MIDI IN. Starts on the DJ's "1".
RESOLUME
Arena or Avenue 7: Preferences → OSC → turn on OSC Input (port 7000). Sets the tempo and resyncs every bar. Optional: launch a column on the drop, or on a new track. Using ABLETON LINK in Resolume? Leave this one off.
GRANDMA3
In MA: Menu → In & Out → OSC: Enable Input, a line with the same port, Receive Command = Yes. Sets a speed master to the DJ's BPM. Optional: Go+ an executor on the drop.
QLAB
QLab 5 (port 53000). Number cues drop (one bar before the chorus), track (a new track on air) and test. Give the box a passcode with Control in Workspace Settings → Network.

Building your own patch? STATUS → OSC OUT sends beat, downbeat, BPM, song section, what's next, the drop and the on-air track to any computer you list.

The timing comes from the master deck's beats over the network. Song sections and the drop need phrase-analysed tracks on recent players.
