HELP / HOW TO

STOP THE MUSIC AT CURFEW

For rooms with a hard music-off time. AUTO-STOP stops every deck at MUSIC OFF, with a countdown on every phone first. It's part of DECK CONTROLS, which stay off until the owner turns them on.

  1. Set tonight's MUSIC OFF: in PLAN A NIGHT, in the venue's usual times (music at this venue), or in HARD STOP.

  2. Owner only, once: unlock with the owner password, then STATUS → DECK CONTROLS → TURN ON.

  3. Each night: DECK CONTROLS → AUTO-STOP AT MUSIC OFF → tap ARM FOR TONIGHT, then hold it 1 second.

At 10, 5 and 1 minutes before, every phone shows a bar with CANCEL AUTO-STOP. Then every deck stops once. It never stops late (if the box was off at the time, it doesn't stop when it comes back), never fights a DJ who presses play again, and resets the next night.

HARD STOP CAN STOP IT (owner): lets the HARD STOP app stop the music in the last 10 minutes too. The app can always cancel.

Stopping a deck over the network sends it back to its cue point. Not every player obeys network commands: try it with your decks before a night that depends on it. See known issues.
