AFTER THE NIGHT

The paperwork and the honest record of the night, made for you from what the decks played and what the box logged.

THE NIGHT REPORT

  1. PLAYED → pick the night → NIGHT REPORT (or NIGHT REPORT on the END OF NIGHT card).

  2. It asks for the password or tonight's crew code once.

  3. PRINT gives a clean black on white page. No PDF needed.

With a plan for the night (GHOST LINK's NIGHTS, or the lineup and cutoff from the HARD STOP app) it shows each DJ's planned start and end next to when their music really started and ended, minutes late and over, and whether MUSIC OFF was kept. With no plan it shows the music half only.

Always: tracks, hours, BPM range, the track that held the air longest, each DJ's numbers, crew calls sent, Wi-Fi drops, dead air, restarts, warnings, notes, and the night's recordings with a download link.

SET INSIGHTS

  1. PLAYED → pick the night → SET INSIGHTS (also at the top of the NIGHT REPORT).

  2. The password or the crew code once. Tonight's, while the music is still on, needs the owner's password.

The night's journey: the tempo track by track, a rough energy line (tempo and the rekordbox phrase mood, a feel, not a measurement), the keys in order, and who was on air when, with each DJ's minutes.

Every change-over in plain words: the tempo (SMOOTH, A STEP UP, A BIG JUMP UP, HALF TIME), the keys (SAME KEY, ONE STEP UP, MINOR TO MAJOR, KEYS APART) and the blend (BLEND 0:42, CUT). HANDOVERS and BIG MOVES narrow the list.

The night against the others: what went out here on earlier nights and when, what each DJ also played on their earlier nights, the most played tracks and the hour they usually go out, and the room's usual tempo hour by hour. Practice nights never count.

On the decks: a loaded track that already went out tonight says PLAYED 23:14. The owner can also turn on PLAYED HERE BEFORE in ALL SETTINGS: a loaded track that went out here on an earlier night then says HERE NOV 12 before it goes on air. Words only, no sound.

THE NIGHT LOG AND + NOTE

The report ends with the NIGHT LOG: sets changing over, crew calls, STAND BY, overruns, restarts, card trouble, Wi-Fi drops and dead air, each with its time. Nobody has to write it.

For what the box can't see: NOW → + NOTE. Pick MEDICAL, SECURITY, TECH, LOST & FOUND or OTHER, add a few words if you want, SAVE. It needs the password or tonight's crew code. Notes stay on the box with that night.

LOG AS CSV and LOG AS TEXT download it for a spreadsheet or an email.

ROYALTY SETLISTS

For the owner (password, not the crew code): at the bottom of the NIGHT REPORT, pick everyone, a DJ, or one set, then a society. Each gives a CSV and a text file. The text file starts with how to submit.

A DJ who played twice in a night gets two sets, split where the decks changed hands, so each can go in as its own performance. Type the venue's full address (street, town, postcode) once in FULL ADDRESS and SAVE: it goes in your downloads and nowhere else.

  • PRS for Music (UK): members report in the Live Reporting Tool in their online account: the venue with its address and postcode, the date, who performed (a DJ set counts), and each work with its duration. Works must be registered with PRS. Small venues: within 12 months. We couldn't confirm a file upload, so type it in from the list.

  • BMI Live (US) and ASCAP OnStage (US): for songwriters who perform their own songs, picked from the society's own list. If the set was other people's records, they probably don't apply. We couldn't see their exact field labels, so the list has what they ask for in plain words.

  • Any other society: everything GHOST LINK knows about each track, for GEMA, SOCAN, APRA AMCOS, a promoter's form or your own records.

GHOST LINK doesn't know writers or publishers and never makes them up. Check the list before you send it. The duration is how long the track was on air, never more than its length.

A DJ with a recording link (HAND IT TO THE DJ) gets the same downloads for their own set on their page, once the night's tracklist is shared, without the venue.

THE MIXCLOUD CHECK

Only for a recorded set. On the DJ's page and in the report, one line says whether the set fits Mixcloud's rules for a show: up to 4 tracks by one artist (no more than 3 in a row) and up to 3 from one release (no more than 2 in a row). If it doesn't, it says what and that the upload is still fine: Mixcloud may make it subscribers-only. Never a pop-up.

While a set is recording, the same check shows as one quiet line on NOW, for the set so far (after its first 15 minutes).

NO ANALYSIS ON A DECK

A deck playing a streaming track, a file not analysed in rekordbox or a CD shows a small NO ANALYSIS tag. The player sends no waveform, beat grid or phrases for it, so waveforms, lights cues and time left have less to go on. Nothing is broken.
