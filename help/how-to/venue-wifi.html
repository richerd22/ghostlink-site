HELP / HOW TO

ADD A VENUE'S WI-FI

Save it once. Next time the box is in range, it joins by itself, and phones stay on the venue's Wi-Fi with internet.

  1. Be on the dashboard (the GHOST LINK Wi-Fi is fine). Unlock first, if this phone isn't already: STATUS → type the password (or tonight's crew code) where it says password or crew code → UNLOCK.

  2. In STATUS → VENUE: tap NEW for a new venue, or pick it and tap EDIT.

  3. Type the venue's name.

  4. Tap + VENUE WI-FI. Under NEARBY, tap the venue's Wi-Fi: the box fills in its exact name (spaces and slashes count, so "Blue Arch / Upstairs" is not "Upstairs"). Then type the password. Nothing listed yet? Type the name exactly as your phone shows it.

  5. More than one Wi-Fi there (staff, booth)? Tap + VENUE WI-FI again for each.

  6. Tap SAVE.

The box tries it as soon as you save, no restart needed (your phone may drop off the GHOST LINK Wi-Fi for a moment while it looks). Later, if it's ever on its own hotspot with the venue's Wi-Fi in range: STATUS → READY FOR TONIGHT → JOIN VENUE WI-FI NOW. A saved name it can't see is marked in amber, with the nearby name it most likely meant.

The box joins it within about a minute when it's in range. The green light goes solid. Phones then join the venue Wi-Fi and open ghostlink.local or gl.cold.fyi.

Passwords stay on the box and only show on unlocked phones. To keep a saved password while editing, leave the password box blank.

THE WI-FI TELLS IT WHERE TONIGHT IS

On a venue's saved Wi-Fi, the box knows where it is. It's careful about it:

  • Only the exact network name you saved counts. "Blue Arch / 51" saved for THE BLUE ARCH means THE BLUE ARCH; "Blue Arch" alone means nothing.
  • Only on the venue's Wi-Fi (not its own GHOST LINK Wi-Fi, not STAND BY), and only after 2 minutes on the same network.
  • Before the music starts, the box sets its venue by itself. Once music is on, or when HARD STOP set tonight somewhere else, it asks instead, at the top of the screen: YOU'RE AT THE BLUE ARCH (Wi-Fi "Blue Arch / 51") - SWITCH TONIGHT? One tap. NOT TONIGHT keeps it quiet for that network until tomorrow.
  • One network saved for two venues (two rooms in one building)? It asks WHICH ONE TONIGHT? with a button for each. It never guesses.
  • A HOME venue's Wi-Fi never pulls a real night away to HOME.
  • Planned tonight at a venue by hand, on a Wi-Fi that isn't saved for it yet? It offers SAVE (and asks once more first), so next time it knows.

With HARD STOP: the app is told the venue and the network name; HARD STOP decides about its own night. GHOST LINK never changes it.

STAND BY: KEEP THE BOX OFF THEIR WI-FI FOR A WHILE

The venue's Wi-Fi acting up, or someone asks? No need to pull the power. Unlocked, go to STATUS → WI-FI → STAND BY · WI-FI QUIET (or tap GHOST LINK at the top → STAND BY). The first line always says where the box shows up right now and since when.

  • STAY OFF THEIR WI-FI: the box leaves the venue's Wi-Fi and becomes its own GHOST LINK network. Phones join that (password on the sticker), or use the DJ cable. It keeps going through a power cycle until its end time.
  • ALL WI-FI OFF: no Wi-Fi from the box at all. Only a laptop on the DJ cable reaches it. It always comes back by itself, and unplugging the power and plugging it back in brings the Wi-Fi straight back.

Then pick how long: 30 MIN, 1 HOUR, TONIGHT (until 6:00 in the morning) or UNTIL I SAY (STAY OFF THEIR WI-FI only). To end it sooner: BACK ON THEIR WI-FI (or JOIN VENUE WI-FI NOW).

While it's on, the decks, recording and the black box keep going. What waits: phones on the venue's Wi-Fi, WATCH FROM HOME, gl.cold.fyi and updates. HARD STOP keeps its own countdown and shows STAND BY; its GHOST LINK info comes back with the Wi-Fi.

When it ends (your tap or its time), the box goes straight back to its saved Wi-Fi, in about 15 seconds. The page says where it's going, for example Going back to "the venue's Wi-Fi" now. If your phone was on the GHOST LINK network, it loses the box at that moment: that's expected. Join that Wi-Fi on your phone and open ghostlink.local. With no saved Wi-Fi around, the box simply stays GHOST LINK. JOIN VENUE WI-FI NOW waits a minute after an end, while the box rejoins by itself.
