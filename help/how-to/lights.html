HELP / HOW TO

LIGHTS

Plug in a cheap LED strip or a DMX light and GHOST LINK drives it from the music: a flash on the beat, a colour from the track's key, a big flash on the drop. Off until the owner turns it on.

WHAT WORKS

  • WLED: an LED strip on a small WLED controller, on the same Wi-Fi as GHOST LINK. The easiest way in.
  • ART-NET or SACN: a DMX node with RGB lights on it (each light 3 channels: red, green, blue) or RGBW lights (4 channels, with white).

Up to 4 lights or strips at once. Pick RGBW next to a light that has a white channel. More lights than fit in one DMX universe? Tick +1 UNIVERSE and the rest carry on in the next one.

SET IT UP

  1. Unlock with the owner's password: STATUS → type the password → UNLOCK.

  2. STATUS → MORE → LIGHTS.

  3. Under WHERE IT GOES, pick WLED, ART-NET or SACN and type the light's address (the WLED app shows it). For ART-NET and SACN, type the universe too.

  4. Type HOW MANY: the pixels on the strip, or the number of RGB lights. For DMX, the DMX START ADDRESS of the first one. SAVE.

  5. Tap TEST. A rainbow runs for 5 seconds. No rainbow? Check the address and that the light is on the same Wi-Fi.

  6. Tap OFF so it says ON.

THE LOOK

  • PULSE ON THE BEAT: a flash on every beat, bigger on the 1.
  • CHASE: light runs along the strip, a step each beat.
  • CALM: a slow colour drift. Good before doors.
  • OFF: dark, apart from the moments below.

The colour follows the key of the track on air. No key: white. BRIGHTEST sets how bright it ever gets (60 % to start). The night presets pick a look too: CLUB NIGHT is PULSE, PRE-DOORS is CALM, HOME PRACTICE is OFF.

Pick a look at a venue and GHOST LINK remembers it there. Next time that's tonight's venue, the card offers it once: USE IT or NOT TONIGHT. It never changes the look by itself.

MOMENTS THAT WIN OVER THE LOOK

  • CREW CALLS: three quick white flashes when a crew call goes.
  • THE DROP: one big flash as the track hits its chorus.
  • SILENCE: a slow red breathe while the music has stopped.
  • HOUSE LOOK: warm and dim after MUSIC OFF, and while GOODNIGHT is up. In the last 10 minutes before MUSIC OFF the look fades down gently.

Each one has its own switch. With HARD STOP, the lights can also follow its countdown.

PHRASE COLOURS (off until you tick it): brighter and whiter in the chorus, softer in the intro and outro. It needs CDJ-3000s and tracks analysed with phrases in rekordbox. Players that don't share phrases (like the XDJ-XZ) just keep the look as it is.

SAFE BY DESIGN

  • BLACKOUT turns every light off at once. Anyone here can press it; ending it needs the crew code or the password.
  • Never more than 3 flashes a second, whatever the tempo.
  • Turning LIGHTS off leaves the lights dark and hands them back to their own app.
