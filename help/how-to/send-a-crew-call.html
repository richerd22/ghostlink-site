HELP / HOW TO

SEND A CREW CALL

A crew call is a short message with a countdown: LIGHTS ON in 2 minutes, THE DROP, LAST CALL, a photo count-in. It shows at the top of every GHOST LINK screen and on the TV view, and goes to any lighting or visuals computer listed in OSC OUT.

  1. Unlock first, if this phone isn't already: STATUS → type the password (or tonight's crew code) → UNLOCK.

  2. Open NOW and tap who you are: LIGHTS, PHOTO, MANAGER... (DJ calls as BOOTH).

  3. Open SEND A CREW CALL at the bottom.

  4. Tap a quick one (THE DROP, LIGHTS ON, LAST CALL, PHOTO COUNT-IN) or type your own.

  5. Pick when: NOW, 10 s, 30 s, 1, 2 or 5 minutes, or type the seconds (up to an hour).

  6. Want the TV to count the last 10 seconds full screen? Tick COUNT-IN.

  7. Tap SEND.

CANCEL ONE

Under LIVE CALLS, tap CANCEL next to it. It goes from every screen at once and its GO never happens.

THE DROP BY ITSELF

The owner can tick THE DROP BY ITSELF: 4 bars before the drop in the track on air, GHOST LINK calls THE DROP with a count-in, landing on the drop. Once per drop. It needs tracks analysed in rekordbox with phrases. Off until the owner turns it on (owner password, not the crew code).

With the HARD STOP app, its panels can show these calls too, and calls made on a panel show here.
