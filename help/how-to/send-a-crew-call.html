HELP / HOW TO

SEND A CREW CALL

A crew call is a short message with a countdown: LIGHTS ON in 2 minutes, THE DROP, LAST CALL, a photo count-in. It shows at the top of every GHOST LINK screen and on the TV view, and goes to any lighting or visuals computer listed in OSC OUT.

  1. Unlock first, if this phone isn't already: STATUS → type the password (or tonight's crew code) → UNLOCK.

  2. Open NOW and tap who you are: LIGHTS, PHOTO, MANAGER... (DJ calls as BOOTH).

  3. Open SEND A CREW CALL at the bottom.

  4. Tap a quick one (THE DROP, LIGHTS ON, LAST CALL, PHOTO COUNT-IN) or type your own.

  5. Pick when: NOW, 10 s, 30 s, 1, 2 or 5 minutes, or type the seconds (up to an hour). Or AT THE DROP (below).

  6. Want the TV to count the last 10 seconds full screen? Tick COUNT-IN.

  7. Tap SEND.

WRAP-UP CALLS

At the top of SEND A CREW CALL: 5 MIN, 1 MIN and WRAP IT. One tap sends WRAP IT UP with that countdown, as a big banner on every GHOST LINK screen (the DJ's phone too), the TV and the STAGE CLOCK. A new one replaces the last. CANCEL is right next to it. No sound.

SEEN, DONE AND URGENT

Every call on the dashboard has SEEN and DONE (no code needed). Whoever sent it sees SEEN 23:41 (BOOTH) under LIVE CALLS. A call nobody has seen after a minute pulses once more on the screens. Tick URGENT before SEND and it goes first, red and bigger, everywhere. More in NIGHT SAFETY.

CANCEL ONE

Under LIVE CALLS, tap CANCEL next to it. It goes from every screen at once and its GO never happens.

AT THE DROP

For CO2, confetti or a strobe that has to land on the drop: tap CO2 (or type your own) and AT THE DROP, then SEND. GHOST LINK holds the call and sends GO on the first beat of the next drop in the track on air. Every screen shows CO2 AT THE DROP · 8 BARS counting down, and the TV counts the last 10 seconds full screen.

If the DJ speeds up, loops or mixes to the other deck first, the call follows the music. If the new track has no drop yet, it waits (it says WAITING) and never goes off at a random moment; after 10 minutes without a drop it's dropped. CANCEL works the whole time.

It needs tracks analysed in rekordbox with phrases. If the track on air has none, GHOST LINK says so and offers NEXT 16-BAR PHRASE or NOW instead. HARD STOP panels can send calls AT THE DROP too.

THE DROP BY ITSELF

The owner can tick THE DROP BY ITSELF: 4 bars before the drop in the track on air, GHOST LINK calls THE DROP with a count-in, landing on the drop. Once per drop. It needs tracks analysed in rekordbox with phrases. Off until the owner turns it on (owner password, not the crew code).

With the HARD STOP app, its panels can show these calls too, and calls made on a panel show here.
