HELP / HOW TO

WHAT KIND OF NIGHT?

GHOST LINK does a lot. Here is the short version for your kind of night: what to plug in, what to tap, and what it does by itself. Everything else can stay off.

On the box: HELP → WHAT CAN IT DO? has the same list. With the HARD STOP app too, its lineup, cutoff and timeline win; without it, everything here still works.

CLUB NIGHT ON CDJs

Plug the box into the DJ switch. Tap STATUS → SETTINGS → CLUB NIGHT, then plan the DJs and MUSIC OFF in NIGHTS. By itself: every deck live, will the next track fit the set, crew calls, the stage clock. Beside your visuals, open the decks in a small window: it keeps the decks in view and shows at most two lines above them. Plan a night · With Resolume

A LAPTOP DJ

Plug the USB sound card into the mixer's spare output. Tap LAPTOP NIGHT. The box hears the BPM and the drop, and can record the set. Laptop DJs

A LINEUP, B2B, CHANGEOVERS

One line per set in NIGHTS: 23:00 TICK b2b LIL TICK. The box switches sets by itself, says when a track runs past the changeover, and RUNNING LATE moves the sets still to come. Name a set

A STAGE WITH AN LED WALL

STATUS → SCREENS: the stage clock for the DJ, NEXT UP for the crowd, the crew screen for the crew. Stage clock

A LIVESTREAM

Add the overlay to OBS, tap START STREAM CLOCK when you go live. HIDE IDS keeps unreleased tracks as ID. After: the setlist with chapter times. With OBS

A WEDDING OR PRIVATE PARTY

Turn on ASK THE DJ with your blocked words. Add the moments in NIGHTS, one a line: 21:30 SPEECHES. The screens say SPEECHES IN 12 in its last half hour. Ask the DJ · Plan a night

OUTDOORS

Set the place in WEATHER. When it gets windy, NOW asks once: HOLD? It never holds by itself. Weather · Night safety

YOU RUN THE ROOM, NOT THE DECKS

MUSIC OFF in NIGHTS, LIGHTS on once. The stage clock counts to the curfew; BLACKOUT is there for anyone. Stop at curfew · Lights

ON TOUR

Pick the venue each night. Each venue remembers its settings and offers them once. BACKUP carries everything to another box. Venue Wi-Fi

AT HOME, RADIO, PODCASTS

HOME PRACTICE keeps the night quiet and never counts it as a show. RECORD a set and take the tracklist with times. Record a set

SOUNDCHECK

PRE-DOORS: the test tones and the PA up front, nothing out to the room yet. Switch to CLUB NIGHT at doors.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG

SHOW STOP puts a message on every screen and the house lights up; HOLD pauses the clocks. Only a person at the venue starts them. Night safety
