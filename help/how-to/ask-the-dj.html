HELP / HOW TO

ASK THE DJ

Song requests from the crowd's phones. They ask and vote; the DJ decides. No money, no sign-up, no pop-ups.

TURN IT ON FOR TONIGHT

  1. Unlock with the owner's password: STATUS → type the password → UNLOCK.

  2. STATUS → ASK THE DJ → TURN ON FOR TONIGHT.

  3. Show the QR. While requests are open, the TV view shows a small ASK THE DJ card with it in the corner. The same QR is in STATUS → ASK THE DJ.

It's off until the owner turns it on, and it closes by itself when the night ends: the next night starts closed. A night preset you saved with it on can open it too.

WHAT GUESTS SEE

  • A song and an artist to type. If you turn on SEARCH THE STICKS, they can pick from the music on the USB sticks in the players instead.
  • Their name and a note, if they want (for a birthday, say). Only the DJ sees those.
  • Everything asked tonight, most votes first, with a vote button. One vote per phone per song.
  • A song asked twice, even spelled a bit differently, becomes one ask with two votes.
  • A song that was already played tonight says so. They can still ask again.
  • Changed their mind? Tap VOTED again to take the vote back. Their own ask with only their vote comes off the list (it asks once first).

To put the QR on the bar or the booth: PRINT A SIGN under ASK THE DJ. It shows the Wi-Fi to join, then the QR. Print it once; it works on any night requests are on.

THE DJ'S LIST

A small ASKS 3 badge sits beside LAYOUT on DECKS (on other tabs, at the end of the tab row). Tap it for the list, most votes first. Each ask has:

  • PLAYING IT: guests see "PLAYING IT SOON".
  • MAYBE LATER: guests see "MAYBE LATER".
  • NOPE: quiet. Guests only ever see "THE DJ SAW IT".

Tap a lit answer again to undo it. When an asked song goes on air, it's marked PLAYED by itself. PAUSE REQUESTS for a break (the list stays), CLEAR (tap twice) to start over. The list never pops up and never makes a sound. It needs the password or tonight's crew code.

GUESTS ON PHONE DATA

The QR works for phones on the venue Wi-Fi or the GHOST LINK hotspot. Most guests are on phone data. For them: ALSO FROM PHONE DATA makes one link for tonight that can only ask and vote, and it works while WATCH FROM HOME is on. The QR switches to it by itself. Anyone with that link can also watch the decks, like any WATCH FROM HOME link. Revoke it any time in SHARE.

KEEPING IT KIND

Slurs, links and rude names or notes are always refused. KEEP IT CLEAN also refuses songs with swear words in the title (off by default: plenty of good songs swear). Each phone gets a few asks every 10 minutes, so nobody floods the list.

BLOCKED WORDS (owner): your own list, one per line, like another club's name. Asks with one of them are turned away politely, without saying which word. Only you see the list.

Want the TV quieter? Turn off QR ON THE TV. "REQUESTS OPEN" ON THE TV adds one small line instead or as well.
