HELP / HOW TO

SHARE LINKS

Private by default. Everywhere when you want it.

Give someone a link instead of the password. It only does what you tick, it ends by itself, and you can take it back at any time. It works with no internet.

  1. Unlock with the owner's password: STATUS → type it → UNLOCK. (Tonight's crew code can't make links.)

  2. STATUS → SHARE → NEW LINK. Or tap GHOST LINK at the top → SHARE LINKS.

  3. Pick one: WATCH, CREW, VJ or MANAGER CAN STOP DECKS. Or tick exactly what it can do.

  4. Type who it's for (like Sam or VJ laptop) and how long it lasts. UNTIL END OF NIGHT is an hour after tonight's music off (or 6 hours if there's no plan).

  5. MAKE LINK, then COPY and send it, or let them scan the QR code.

What a link can do

Everything is off until you tick it.

  • WATCH: the dashboard, read only.
  • CREW CALLS: send and cancel crew calls.
  • STOP / START THE DECKS: only while DECK CONTROLS is on at the box, and only on players that obey. Their phone shows what's playing and who's on before anything is sent.
  • SETTINGS: who's on and tonight's plan.
  • PLAY ON THE PA: for the HARD STOP app when it isn't on the venue Wi-Fi. It plays what HARD STOP may play, only while LET HARD STOP CALL THE PA is on at the box. Never EMERGENCY.
  • ALSO FROM OUTSIDE THE VENUE: the controls above also work from anywhere, through WATCH FROM HOME. Without it, a link only watches from outside.

A link can never touch Wi-Fi, passwords, updates or backups, and can't start a recording (stopping one is fine).

Take it back

STATUS → SHARE lists every link: who, what, until when, last used. REVOKE (tap twice) ends one at once.

REMOTE OFF works only on the box's own network: OFF FROM OUTSIDE refuses every link from outside the venue; ALL LINKS OFF refuses all of them. It always wins.

Everything done with a link shows in NETWORK → EVENTS with the link's name, and in the black box.

Claim this box

STATUS → SHARE → CLAIM THIS BOX shows a one-time code (10 minutes) for linking the box to a GHOST LINK account. Your box keeps working without an account.
