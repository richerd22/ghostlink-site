SELLING OR HANDING ON A GHOST LINK

Selling your GHOST LINK, or giving it to someone? GET READY TO SELL puts the box back to new, so the next owner starts fresh and nothing of yours goes with it.

  1. Unlock with the owner's password: STATUS → type the password → UNLOCK. Tonight's crew code can't do this.

  2. STATUS → BACKUP → BACKUP first, if you'd like to keep your venues and nights for a box of your own later. The sheet has a DOWNLOAD A BACKUP FIRST button too.

  3. STATUS → GET READY TO SELL. The sheet lists what goes and what stays.

  4. Pick ERASE THE NIGHTS (picked for you: right when it goes to someone else) or KEEP THE NIGHTS (only if the box stays with you or your crew). Then NEXT.

  5. Type the owner's password, then the box's short name, then tap GET READY TO SELL. Or skip the name and hold the button for 3 seconds.

  6. GHOST LINK restarts as a new box (about 20 seconds). Not while a deck is playing or a set is recording.

WHAT GOES

  • Your password. The box goes back to the password it shipped with, and the next owner has to pick their own before they can change anything.
  • Signed-in phones, the crew code, share links, and the box's id. It gets a new id and leaves your account.
  • The unit's short name and number.
  • Venues, with their saved Wi-Fi passwords. The box forgets that Wi-Fi and shows its own GHOST LINK network.
  • Planned nights, USB stick names, show control, sounds you added, your own night presets and every other setting. A settings file you saved first (MOVE SETTINGS TO ANOTHER UNIT) puts them on your next box without any passwords.
  • Lights, visuals cues, API keys, sheet links, the crew room's keys, the weather place, the curfew ladder, the door count, song requests, hidden IDs, set notes, the stream clock, running late, and what kind of nights you picked. The next owner gets the new-box walk.
  • The black box's lines. One line is left, saying when the box was reset.
  • With ERASE THE NIGHTS: PLAYED, every night's history, recordings, records and arcade scores.

WHAT STAYS

  • GHOST LINK itself and its updates.
  • The stock sound packs.
  • The DJ cable connection.

YOUR ACCOUNT

If the box was on your account, it tells the account it's gone. When it can't (no internet), the sheet says so: remove it yourself at hardstop.cold.fyi/account (RELEASE THIS BOX). The box doesn't need an account to work.

THE NEXT OWNER

Join the GHOST LINK Wi-Fi (the password is on the box's sticker), open the dashboard and pick a password on STATUS. That's it.
