HELP / HOW TO

HAND OVER: SELLING IT, GIVING IT AWAY, STARTING OVER

Selling your GHOST LINK, giving it to someone, or starting over yourself? HAND OVER puts the box back to new, so the next owner starts fresh and nothing of yours goes with it. (It used to be called GET READY TO SELL. It's the same thing.)

  1. Unlock with the owner's password: STATUS → type the password → UNLOCK. Tonight's crew code can't do this.

  2. STATUS → BACKUP → BACKUP first, if you'd like to keep your venues and nights for a box of your own later. The sheet has a DOWNLOAD A BACKUP FIRST button too.

  3. STATUS → MORE → HAND OVER (or YOUR ACCOUNT → HAND OVER). The sheet lists what goes and what stays.

  4. THE NIGHTS: ERASE THE NIGHTS (picked for you: right when it goes to someone else) or KEEP THE NIGHTS (only if the box stays with you or your crew).

  5. THE MUSIC: CLEAR IT (picked for you) or KEEP MY MUSIC. GHOST LINK never copies your library: it reads each USB stick fresh. What it remembers is whose stick is whose. Keep that only when you're starting over with your own sticks. Then NEXT.

  6. Type the owner's password, then the box's short name, then tap HAND OVER. Or skip the name and hold the button for 3 seconds.

  7. GHOST LINK restarts at first-time setup (about 20 seconds). If the box was in your account, the last screen says ALSO LET IT GO IN YOUR ACCOUNT with the link: tap it and let the box go there too.

Never during a night: not while a deck is playing, a set is recording or music is coming in on the sound card, and not from 2 hours before tonight's planned start until an hour after MUSIC OFF.

WHAT GOES

  • The box's key. It gets a new one, so every link it made before stops working, and your account no longer counts it as yours.
  • Your password. The box goes back to the password it shipped with, and the next owner picks their own before they can change anything.
  • The paired phones (signed-in phones) and the crew code.
  • The HARD STOP link: the crew room's keys and its internet room invite, and the hub key.
  • Share links, the unit's short name and number.
  • Venues, with their saved Wi-Fi passwords. The box forgets that Wi-Fi and shows its own GHOST LINK network.
  • Planned nights, show control, sounds you added, your own night presets and every other setting. A settings file you saved first (MOVE SETTINGS TO ANOTHER UNIT) puts them on your next box without any passwords.
  • Lights, visuals cues, API keys, sheet links, the weather place, the curfew ladder, the door count, song requests, hidden IDs, set notes, the stream clock, running late, and what kind of nights you picked.
  • Whose USB stick is whose, unless you picked KEEP MY MUSIC.
  • The black box's lines. One line is left: HANDED OVER, and when.
  • With ERASE THE NIGHTS: PLAYED, every night's history, recordings, records and arcade scores.

WHAT STAYS

  • GHOST LINK itself and its updates.
  • The stock sound packs.
  • The DJ cable connection.

YOUR ACCOUNT

If the box was in your account, it tells the account to let it go. When it can't (no internet), the last screen says so and gives you the link: open your account and tap SELLING IT? on the box. Selling it to someone? Let it go in your account first, and the buyer's claim goes through at once. The box doesn't need an account to work. More: KEEP IT IN YOUR ACCOUNT.

THE NEXT OWNER

Join the GHOST LINK Wi-Fi (the password is on the box's sticker), open the dashboard, and the box walks them through setup. Bought one? BOUGHT ONE USED.
