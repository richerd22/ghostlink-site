HELP / HOW TO

PLAY BOO'S ARCADE

BOO is the ghost. Small games played on the music that's actually on, with one high-score board for every phone on the box.

  1. STATUS → TOOLS → BOO'S ARCADE. No password.

  2. Pick a game. Type three or more letters for the board.

RIDE THE WAVE
BOO surfs the playing track's waveform at its tempo. Jump on the beat.
ON THE ONE
Tap along to the room's beat.
EQ KILL
Kill the bass before the drop, bring it back on the drop.
BEATMATCH
Line up two beat grids.
CATCH THE DROP
Tap right on the drop.

TONIGHT ON A SCREEN shows tonight's best scores with a code to scan, for a screen by the bar. No sound, ever: it sits next to a loud booth.
