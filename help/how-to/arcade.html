HELP / HOW TO

PLAY BOO'S ARCADE

BOO is the ghost. Small games played on the music that's actually on, with one high-score board for every phone on the box.

  1. On NOW, tap BOO'S ARCADE at the bottom (every role but DJ). Or type arcade in GO TO, or find it in STATUS → TOOLS. No password.

  2. Getting a friend in: tap GHOST LINK at the top, then SHOW ITS CODE, and they scan it.

  3. Pick a game. Each one starts with one line on how to play.

  4. Type a name for the board: 3 to 12 letters or numbers.

RIDE THE WAVE
BOO surfs the playing track. Hold to rise, let go to fall, and stay on the dotted line. It arcs over the red spikes.
CALL THE DROP
The bars count down to the track's next change, then go dark. Tap on the 1. The drop scores double.
ON THE ONE
Tap along to the room's beat.
EQ KILL
Kill the bass before the drop, bring it back on the drop.
BEATMATCH
Line up two beat grids.
CATCH THE DROP
Tap right on the drop.
ON GO
A countdown that goes dark at the end. Tap on GO. While a crew call is counting down, it becomes a round worth double.

More in the menu: TAP THE TEMPO, HIGHER OR LOWER, SET LIST, CAMELOT, and DAILY RIDE (the same course for everyone today).

Every game starts easy. RIDE THE WAVE has nothing to hit for the first 8 beats, and your first ride on a phone is a short lesson. Elsewhere a first miss or a tap that's far too early won't end it.

TONIGHT ON A SCREEN shows tonight's best scores with a code to scan, for a screen by the bar. No sound, ever: it sits next to a loud booth.
