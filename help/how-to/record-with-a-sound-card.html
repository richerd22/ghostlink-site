RECORD THE MIX WITH A USB SOUND CARD

A small USB sound card lets GHOST LINK hear the mixer: a level meter, a warning if the room goes silent, and set recordings when the DJ says yes. Setting it up takes about five minutes. The dashboard walks you through it.

Just plugged the card in? A small SOUND CARD IN · ● RECORD · SET UP shows at the top of every screen for a minute, even the small window. RECORD starts recording this set (password or crew code); SET UP opens the guide. If a recording ever hears nothing while the decks play, the same spot says RECORDING BUT HEARING NOTHING · CHECK THE CABLE.

When the box sees a sound card, a quiet line shows at the top of the dashboard: SOUND CARD FOUND · SET UP. Tap SET UP for the guide below, or × to hide it for good. Know what you're doing? Tap DONE on any step. The guide is always under NETWORK → SOUND IN → SET UP GUIDE.

  1. PLUG THE MIXER INTO THE CARD

    The sound card goes in any USB port on the box. It shows up within 5 seconds.

    The guide picks your mixer from the DJ network, or from the venue's gear notes, or the one picked here last time (or tap yours: the usual ones first, then MORE) and says which output to use (table below). Cable from that output to the card's INPUT, never OUTPUT. Red is right, white is left.

  2. PLAY SOMETHING

    Play a track. The meter moves and a tick says SOUND IS COMING IN. Aim for the loud parts around -12 dB.

    The card has no input knob: the level comes from the mixer. The guide says what to turn if it's too quiet, hitting red, or only one side moves (a loose plug).

  3. MAKE A TEST RECORDING

    TEST 10 SECONDS, then press play and listen on the phone. The test stays in the box's memory only: never saved, gone in 10 minutes. It needs the password or tonight's crew code.

  4. DONE

    The meter and the SILENCE warning just work from now on. Recording a set is separate, and always the DJ's call: record a set.

WHICH OUTPUT ON YOUR MIXER

DJM-A9, V10, 900NXS2
REC OUT (RCA, no knob of its own) → RCA to RCA cable. Taken, say by someone's recorder? MASTER 2 right next to it (RCA): fine for recording, it's louder and follows the MASTER knob. Then BOOTH (two 1/4-inch jacks), if the booth monitors aren't on it.
DJM-S11, S9, S7, 750MK2
No REC OUT. MASTER 2 (RCA) → RCA to RCA cable; it follows MASTER. Taken? BOOTH (two 1/4-inch jacks) with its own knob, if the booth monitors aren't on it.
XDJ-XZ
No REC OUT. MASTER 2 (RCA) → RCA to RCA cable; it follows MASTER LEVEL. Taken? BOOTH (two 1/4-inch jacks) → 2 x 1/4-inch to 2 x RCA cable, set with the BOOTH LEVEL knob without changing the room.
OTHER MIXERS
REC OUT if it has one (it's made for recording). If not: a spare RCA master out, or BOOTH. Never the headphone jack, DIGITAL OUT or SEND.

Then tap the jack you used under WHICH JACK? in the guide: the too hot and too quiet warnings name it and the knob that sets it. Every output busy? A Y splitter on REC OUT (or the RCA master) shares it with the box.

Every mixer, all-in-one and controller, every jack, best first: mixers and gear.

From each maker's published manuals. If your mixer's back panel looks different, trust the labels on it.

TOO QUIET OR TOO LOUD

NOTHING MOVES
Is the cable in the card's INPUT? In the right output on the mixer? Channel fader and crossfader up?
ONE SIDE ONLY
One plug is loose or in the wrong jack.
HITTING RED
On a booth or zone out: turn its own knob down a little. On MASTER 2: it follows the MASTER knob, so the master is hot; turning it down turns the room down too. On REC OUT (no knob): the DJ's mix is hot; a few red flashes are fine.
QUIET
Turn the output's level knob up (BOOTH or MASTER). REC OUT follows the mix. A quiet recording is still usable; it can be turned up later.
