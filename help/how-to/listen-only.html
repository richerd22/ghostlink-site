HELP / HOW TO

MAKE IT GO QUIET

If anything seems off during a set and you want GHOST LINK out of the way, right now:

  1. STATUS → CONTROLS → LISTEN ONLY. No password needed.

  2. GHOST LINK leaves the DJ network. Decks keep showing what it last saw.

  3. To go back: REJOIN. Decks are live again in about 15 seconds.

Last resort: pull the power. Nothing breaks, and the booth doesn't notice.
