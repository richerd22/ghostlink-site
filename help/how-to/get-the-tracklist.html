HELP / HOW TO

GET THE TRACKLIST OR PLAYLIST

  1. Open PLAYED. Pick the night from the menu at the top (tonight is first).

  2. Tap what you need:

PLAYLIST
Downloads a playlist file (.m3u8). Import it into rekordbox or Serato.
COPY TRACKLIST
Copies the list with times from the start of each set. Paste into Mixcloud or 1001Tracklists.
COPY SETLIST
Copies the list as plain text.
CSV
A spreadsheet, with who played each track.
RECAP IMAGE
A picture of the night to post.

Each set is its own block, and SOUNDCHECK is kept apart, so a DJ gets just their set.
