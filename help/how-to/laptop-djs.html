LAPTOP DJS: BPM BY EAR

A DJ playing from a laptop (Serato, rekordbox on a laptop, Traktor) sends nothing over DJ Link, so the decks stay empty. With a USB sound card on the mixer, GHOST LINK listens instead.

  1. Plug a USB sound card into the box and the mixer's REC OUT into the card. Which plug on your mixer.

  2. When no DJ Link deck is playing and the card hears a beat, BY EAR · ~124 BPM shows by itself in about 10 seconds: on DECKS, NOW, TV MODE, the VISUALIZER and NOW PLAYING.

  3. It shows the beat, and BREAKDOWN, BUILD and THE DROP as they happen.

  4. As soon as a DJ Link deck plays, it goes away and the deck shows as usual.

A WHOLE NIGHT BY EAR

No DJ Link all night (a laptop set, a cable that won't link, a battle mixer)? The sound card still keeps the night:

  • TONIGHT BY EAR on NOW and DECKS: music on or off and for how long, the BPM (one decimal, like 136.9, once it's steady; ~137 before), the big drops, the line's loudness (LUFS), the room level if a HARD STOP panel sends one, and which part of the night it is.
  • Parts: a silence of 30 seconds or more, or the tempo moving and staying, starts a new part. If tonight's lineup has set times, each part gets the DJ's name from it.
  • Recording? Each big drop says how far into the recording it was, so it's easy to find later.
  • END OF NIGHT shows the night by ear: hours of music, BPM range, drops, loudness, the parts, and the last night that was heard this way. HARD STOP gets PLAYING / QUIET and the BPM by ear too.

Never a track title: the sound can't tell it, so GHOST LINK doesn't guess. Tap any number on TONIGHT BY EAR to see where it comes from.

As exact as you like: STATUS → DISPLAY (THIS PHONE) → BPM shows 137, 136.9 or 136.90 on this phone (two decimals only from the decks: the ear stays at one). ALL SETTINGS → BPM BY EAR: ACCURATE (the default, one decimal after about 15 seconds) or FAST (sooner, a little rougher).

WHAT IT CAN'T KNOW

It's an estimate from the sound, so it says ~ and BY EAR until it's steady. No track title, key or waveform. Steady four-to-the-floor music reads right to the BPM; swung or half-time drums can read slower for a while. Where the 1 of the bar is shows only after a drop. During a long blend of two tempos it keeps the old one until the new one is clear.

TURN IT OFF

NETWORK → SOUND IN → LISTEN FOR BPM WHEN NO DECKS (laptop DJs): OFF. Needs the owner's password. It never records, sends or plays anything either way.
