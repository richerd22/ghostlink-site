HELP / HOW TO

WEATHER

For outdoor nights: the wind, the gusts and thunder in the next hour, as a small line on the stage clock, the TV and the crew screen. When it gets past your numbers, NOW asks once: HOLD?

Off until the owner turns it on. It needs the box on a Wi-Fi with internet.

TURN IT ON

  1. STATUS → NIGHT SAFETY → WEATHER (the owner's password).

  2. Type the nearest town and tap FIND, then pick the right one. Or type the latitude and longitude, like 42.36, -71.06.

  3. Tap WEATHER: ON.

While the night is on (from two hours before it starts until an hour after MUSIC OFF, or while music plays) the box asks for the forecast every 10 minutes. The stage clock and the TV show WIND 32 · GUSTS 61 km/h, plus THUNDER when a storm is due. Units: km/h or mph, AUTO picks by the box's time zone. The NIGHT REPORT keeps the worst gust and any thunder of the night.

WHERE THE PLACE GOES

The place stays on the box. It goes only to Open-Meteo (free weather), as its latitude and longitude: the name is looked up once, when you tap FIND. It never goes to HARD STOP, a share link, a backup or a settings file. FORGET IT takes it off the box.

HOLD?

When the gusts in the next hour reach your number, or thunder is due, NOW and the crew screen show one calm line: GUSTS 70 km/h IN THE NEXT HOUR: HOLD? Tap HOLD to open HOLD with WEATHER (or LIGHTNING) already picked, then HOLD THE SHOW if you agree. NOT NOW hides it for 30 minutes (it comes back sooner if the gusts get worse). GHOST LINK never holds the show by itself: a person decides. More about HOLD.

  • Gusts: 50 km/h (31 mph) to start. Your stage, tent or truss supplier has a wind plan with its own numbers: use theirs.
  • Thunder: on to start. The usual rule is everyone in at the first thunder and 30 minutes after the last; HOLD LIGHTNING counts those 30 minutes down.

Each venue remembers its gust number: change it on a night at that venue, and next time the card asks LAST TIME AT THIS VENUE: GUSTS 40 km/h with USE IT or NOT TONIGHT. It never changes by itself.

NO INTERNET

If the box can't reach the forecast, the line says NO WEATHER (OFFLINE) once, then nothing, until a reading comes back. An old reading is never shown as if it were new.

A forecast is a forecast. Keep an eye on the sky and follow the venue's and the stage supplier's weather plan. With the HARD STOP app, it gets the same wind, gusts and storm (never the place).
