STAGE CLOCK

A big clock for an LED wall or a monitor the DJ can see from the decks: how long is left in the set, when it ends, who's on and who's next, the changeover between sets, and the time. It reads from across the room.

  1. Plan tonight's set times: STATUS → NIGHTS → PLAN A NIGHT (who plays when, and MUSIC OFF if there is one).

  2. On the computer or TV stick that drives the screen, open GHOST LINK and go to STATUS → SCREENS → STAGE CLOCK.

  3. Tap or click it once to go full screen. It keeps the screen awake. It works the long way and the tall way.

WHAT THE COLOURS MEAN

  • White: plenty of time.
  • Amber: 10 minutes or less (it says 10 MIN WARNING).
  • Red: 5 minutes or less (5 MIN WARNING).
  • Red, slowly dimming: the last minute (LAST MINUTE). Never a strobe; screens set to reduce motion stay still.
  • Red with a +: the set has ended and the music is still going. It counts up: +0:42 OVER.

Between two sets with a gap, it shows CHANGEOVER and counts down to the next set. Before the first set it counts down to it. After the night it says THAT'S THE NIGHT and shows the time.

It only says OVER when it can tell: music is on air and GHOST LINK still has the last DJ on the decks. Silence never counts as over.

NO SET TIMES YET

With nothing planned, the stage clock is a calm clock that says NO SET TIMES YET, with where to set them. A MUSIC OFF time on its own is enough: it counts down to that.

WRAP-UP CALLS

Someone at the booth can tell the DJ to wrap up without walking over: NOW → SEND A CREW CALL → 10 MIN, 5 MIN, 1 MIN or WRAP IT. A big banner shows on the stage clock and on every GHOST LINK screen, the DJ's phone too. Other crew calls show small under it. More about crew calls.

HOLD AND SHOW STOP

During a HOLD (weather, a stage fix) the clock says HOLD: WEATHER and the set's countdown stands still; MUSIC OFF stays put, so a last set that ends at MUSIC OFF keeps counting. LIGHTNING shows ALL CLEAR IN 23:10. A SHOW STOP turns the whole screen red with its message. With the DOOR COUNT on, a small IN 412 / 450 sits in the corner. NIGHT SAFETY.

To start a HOLD from the clock itself, tap the screen: HOLD: WEATHER · LIGHTNING · STAGE FIX · SAFETY shows for a few seconds, one tap each (or RESUME while a HOLD is up). It asks for the crew code the first time on that screen.

SET NOTES

A short note for the next act, for the crew: type it after the name in NIGHTS, like 23:30 LIL TICK // NEEDS TT + MIC (40 letters). In the last 15 minutes of a set and in the changeover, the stage clock and NOW show NEXT: LIL TICK · NEEDS TT + MIC.

NEXT UP: A SCREEN FOR THE CROWD

STATUS → SCREENS → NEXT UP (or stage.html?view=crowd): a friendly screen for an LED wall or a screen by the bar. Who's on now, who's next and when, and the countdown in a changeover. No warnings, no overtime, no crew calls or notes. During a HOLD it says A SHORT PAUSE; a SHOW STOP shows its message. Add &weather=1 for the wind line, and &rotate=90 for a sideways screen.

WEATHER

With WEATHER on, a small line at the top: WIND 32 · GUSTS 61 km/h.

ALSO

  • TV MODE can open it too: add ?clock=1 to the TV MODE address.
  • A screen mounted on its side: STATUS → SCREENS → STAGE CLOCK SIDEWAYS. Upside down or the other way: add ?rotate=180 or 270 to the address. With a keyboard on the screen's computer, R turns it a quarter each time.
  • No track titles are shown: only DJ names and times.

With the HARD STOP app, its lineup and end times are used when the night isn't planned in GHOST LINK, and its set notes show the same way.
