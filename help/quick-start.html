QUICK START

Five steps. Under two minutes to read.

  1. PLUG IN

    Run a network cable from the box to a spare port on the DJ network: the switch the players plug into, or the venue's DJ router. Not straight into one player: then it only sees that one player.

  2. POWER

    Plug in the box's own power supply. Give it a minute. It finds the players and the mixer by itself.

  3. JOIN THE WI-FI

    On your phone, join the Wi-Fi called GHOST LINK (the password is on the sticker). The dashboard pops up: tap GOT IT. If the box already knows the venue Wi-Fi, stay on that Wi-Fi and open ghostlink.local or gl.cold.fyi instead.

  4. CHECK

    Open STATUS. BOOTH CHECK should say DJ CABLE IN, JOINED AS DEVICE (a number from 7 to 15) and your players. Load a track: the title, art and waveform show on DECKS.

  5. SHARE

    Tap GHOST LINK at the top of the screen. You get the address on this Wi-Fi, a QR code and COPY. Anyone on the same Wi-Fi can open it. No app.

That's it. At the end of the night, just unplug it.

OPTIONAL TONIGHT

  • Recording a set the DJ agreed to: record a set.
  • Lights or visuals following the DJ: hook up lights and visuals.
  • A hard curfew: stop the music at curfew.
  • Crew who need the controls: give the crew code.

THE GREEN LIGHT

SOLID
On a Wi-Fi it knows (the venue's, or yours).
SLOW BLINK
It is the GHOST LINK Wi-Fi. Join that.
FAST BLINK
No Wi-Fi yet. Wait about 90 seconds.
