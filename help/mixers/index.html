MIXERS AND GEAR

Find your mixer: where the USB sound card plugs in, and what GHOST LINK can see with it. The dashboard says the same: tap WHICH MIXER? on the No mixer on the DJ network warning, or open SET UP SOUND IN.

THE SHORT VERSION

BEST FIRST
A record output first (REC OUT, RECORD, SESSION, TAPE): it's made for recording. Taken? A spare master out (MASTER 2, the RCA one). Then BOOTH, if the booth monitors aren't on it. The big XLR master is the PA's: only with the venue's OK.
IT'S TAKEN
Someone's recorder on REC OUT is normal. Use the next one down the list. MASTER 2 is fine for recording: it's louder, and it moves with the MASTER knob.
NEVER
The headphone jack (it carries what the DJ cues), DIGITAL OUT (the USB sound card can't read digital), SEND (the DJ's effects), USB (the DJ's laptop).
TELL GHOST LINK
In SET UP SOUND IN, tap the jack the cable is in under WHICH JACK?. The too hot and too quiet warnings then name it and the knob that sets it. Not tapped: they say "the output GHOST LINK is plugged into".
LEVELS
No knob: the loud parts sit wherever the mix sits. Follows MASTER: turning the room up turns the recording up. Own knob: set the recording with it; the room stays the same.
LINK PORT
A mixer with a LINK (network) port joins the DJ network with the players. Cable it to the same switch and GHOST LINK sees ON AIR: which deck is out loud. No LINK port: titles, art, waveforms, BPM, PLAYED (guessed from the MASTER deck) and SOUND IN still work.

LAPTOP DJs (SERATO, REKORDBOX, TRAKTOR, VIRTUALDJ)

A laptop on a controller sends nothing GHOST LINK can read: no titles, decks or ON AIR from it. What a laptop night gets, with the USB sound card on a spare output of the mixer or controller: the level meter, the SILENCE warning, recording the set, and BY EAR (the BPM, the beat and the drop heard from the sound, an estimate). Any CDJs on the DJ network still show as usual.

CDJs WITH NO MIXER ON THE DJ NETWORK

Fine to play like this. Everything in NO LINK PORT above applies. To get ON AIR back, the mixer needs a LINK port cabled to the same switch as the players.

NOT LISTED?

Look on the back for REC OUT (or RECORD, SESSION, TAPE), then MASTER 2, then BOOTH. Never PHONES, DIGITAL OUT or SEND. Look for a socket marked LINK or LAN for the DJ network. Pick NOT LISTED in the dashboard for the general advice.

From each maker's published manuals (rear panel, specs, block diagrams), read in November 2026. Where a detail couldn't be confirmed, it says check. Panels change between versions: trust the labels on your mixer.
