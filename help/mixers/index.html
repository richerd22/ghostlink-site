MIXERS AND GEAR

Find your mixer: where the USB sound card plugs in, and what GHOST LINK can see with it. The dashboard says the same: tap WHICH MIXER? on the No mixer on the DJ network warning, or open SET UP SOUND IN.

THE SHORT VERSION

LINK PORT
A mixer with a LINK (network) port joins the DJ network with the players. Cable it to the same switch and GHOST LINK sees ON AIR: which deck is out loud.
NO LINK PORT
Fine to play. Titles, art, waveforms, BPM, PLAYED (guessed from the MASTER deck; a track counts after 30 seconds) and SOUND IN still work. Missing: ON AIR and fader info, and the dead-air warning goes by play state.
SOUND CARD
A record output first (REC OUT, RECORD, SESSION, TAPE): it's made for recording. None: a spare RCA master out (MASTER 2), or BOOTH. Never the headphone jack: it carries what the DJ cues, not the mix.
LEVELS
No knob of its own: the loud parts sit wherever the mix sits. Follows MASTER: turning the room up turns the recording up. Own BOOTH knob: set the recording with it; the room stays the same.

CONTROLLERS

BOOTH (1/4-inch jacks, level knob: check) (or the RCA master if the PA is on another pair (check the back panel). A laptop controller: nothing to read.

LAPTOP DJs (SERATO, REKORDBOX, TRAKTOR, VIRTUALDJ)

A laptop on a controller sends nothing GHOST LINK can read: no titles, decks or ON AIR from it. What a laptop night gets, with the USB sound card on a spare output of the mixer or controller: the level meter, the SILENCE warning, recording the set, and BY EAR (the BPM, the beat and the drop heard from the sound, an estimate). Any CDJs on the DJ network still show as usual.

CDJs WITH NO MIXER ON THE DJ NETWORK

Fine to play like this. Everything in NO LINK PORT above applies. To get ON AIR back, the mixer needs a LINK port cabled to the same switch as the players.

NOT LISTED?

Look on the back for REC OUT (or RECORD, SESSION, TAPE), then MASTER 2, then BOOTH. Look for a socket marked LINK or LAN for the DJ network. Pick NOT LISTED in the dashboard for the general advice.
