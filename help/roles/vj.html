FOR VISUALS

The tempo, the bar and what's coming, on a phone or straight into your software.

  • NOW → VJ: track, BPM, beat counter, section, next section, key.
  • Resolume and other apps follow the DJ with ABLETON LINK, or the RESOLUME preset: hook up lights and visuals.
  • A small browser window beside Resolume: DECKS → LAYOUT → STACKED. Every deck fits, and the one playing out loud gets the most room. On a touchscreen, swipe sideways to change tabs.
  • STATUS → SCREENS → STREAM OVERLAY: a see-through now-playing card for OBS. WHAT'S ON: see-through text with who's on and who's next.
  • Fire a column on every crew call's GO, or at music off: SHOW CONTROL → your app → SET UP (blank = off).
