FOR THE DJ

Nothing to do: play like you always do. If you want to look, here's what helps.

  • NOW → DJ: track, time left, BPM, key, the section, set times, all decks.
  • On a cued deck, MIX 8A→9A ✓ +2.1% says whether the key fits and the tempo change it needs.
  • PLAYED 23:14 in amber: already played tonight. PLAYED HERE: played in this room before.
  • Your tracklist: PLAYED → COPY TRACKLIST or PLAYLIST (how). It only goes public if you say so (how).
  • A recording of your set only happens if someone taps ● RECORD for it, and a red ● REC shows at the top while it does (how).
  • Sharing a USB or going B2B? Tell the booth whose USB is whose (how).

The box doesn't touch the mixer or the sound. Its deck controls stay off unless the box's owner turns them on.
