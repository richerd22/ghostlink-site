FOR LIGHTS

The DJ's tempo and bar, for your eyes or your desk.

  • NOW → LIGHTS: BPM, big beat squares, the section and the next one.
  • Into the desk: MIDI CLOCK (with a USB MIDI interface), GRANDMA3 (a speed master to the DJ's BPM), QLAB cues, or OSC. See hook up lights and visuals.
  • A cue on the drop: GRANDMA3 Go+ an executor, QLab cue drop, or OSC /ghostlink/drop, one bar before the chorus.

Sections and the drop need phrase-analysed tracks on recent players.
