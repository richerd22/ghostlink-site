FOR THE TECH

The booth at a glance, and what to do when something's off.

  • STATUS → BOOTH CHECK before doors: clock, venue, Wi-Fi, DJ cable, players, mixer, power. See quick start.
  • NOW → TECH: network health, all decks, time left, set times.
  • Alerts on every phone: DEAD AIR, a deck ending with nothing loaded, a USB pulled, FX LEFT ON, SILENCE (with a sound card).
  • GEAR CARE · FYI: a trim or EQ near the top, the master near max, a low-quality file on air.
  • Something weird: LISTEN ONLY, no password (how). A deck stuck on LOADING: restart reading.
  • Add-ons: gear to buy. Recording: record a set. Curfew: stop the music at curfew.
